=== a1-my-morning | My Morning | Daily Life | A1
I get up at seven o'clock every day. First, I open the window and look at the sky. Then I wash my face and brush my teeth. I put on my clothes and go to the kitchen.

For breakfast, I eat bread with butter and drink a cup of hot tea. My cat sits next to me and waits for milk. I give her a little milk in a small blue bowl.

At eight o'clock, I take my bag and walk to the bus stop. The bus is often late, but I am never in a hurry. I like my quiet mornings.

=== a1-my-family | My Family | Family | A1
My name is Sam, and I am ten years old. I live in a small house with my family. There are five people in my family: my mom, my dad, my two sisters, and me.

My mom is a teacher. She is kind and very funny. My dad is a cook in a big restaurant. He makes great pizza on Sundays.

My sisters are Lucy and Kate. Lucy is twelve, and Kate is six. Lucy likes books, and Kate likes dolls. We play games together in the evening. I love my family.

=== a1-the-red-bike | The Red Bike | Short Fiction | A1
Tom has a new bike. It is red and shiny, and it has a small bell. Tom is very happy. He rides his bike in the park every afternoon.

One day, Tom sees a little girl in the park. She is sad because she has no bike. Her name is Mia. She watches the other children and does not smile.

Tom stops and rings his bell. "Do you want to ride my bike?" he asks. Mia smiles. She rides around the park two times. Now Tom and Mia are friends, and they share the red bike.

=== a1-at-the-market | At the Market | Shopping | A1
On Saturday morning, Anna goes to the market with her grandmother. The market is big and busy. There are many people and many colors. Some people sell fruit, and some people sell fresh flowers.

Anna and her grandmother buy apples, bananas, and tomatoes. They also buy a big piece of cheese. The cheese man is friendly. He gives Anna a small piece to try. It is delicious.

At the end, Anna's grandmother buys yellow flowers for the kitchen table. They walk home slowly with two heavy bags. Anna loves Saturday mornings at the market.

=== a1-my-dog-max | My Dog Max | Animals | A1
I have a dog, and his name is Max. He is three years old. Max is big and brown, and he has long ears. He is very friendly, and he loves people.

Every morning, I take Max for a walk. We go to the park near my house. Max runs fast and plays with a ball. He likes other dogs, but he does not like cats.

In the evening, Max sleeps on the floor in the living room. Sometimes he sleeps on my bed, but my mom does not like that. Max is my best friend.

=== a1-a-rainy-day | A Rainy Day | Weather | A1
Today it is raining. The sky is gray, and the streets are wet. I can hear the rain on the window. It is cold outside, so I stay at home.

I make a cup of hot chocolate and sit on the sofa. I have a good book and a warm blanket. My little brother plays with his cars on the floor.

In the afternoon, the rain stops. The sun comes out, and there is a rainbow in the sky. My brother and I put on our boots and go outside. We jump in the big puddles and laugh.

=== a1-my-room | My Room | Home | A1
My room is small, but I like it very much. The walls are light green, and there is a big window. From the window, I can see a tall tree and the street.

There is a bed, a desk, and a chair in my room. On the desk, there is a lamp and my computer. I do my homework here every day. There are many books on the shelf.

I have a soft carpet on the floor. It is blue and white. My favorite thing in my room is a photo of my grandparents. It is on the wall next to my bed.

=== a1-lunch-at-school | Lunch at School | School | A1
At school, we have lunch at twelve o'clock. The lunchroom is big and noisy. All the students sit at long tables and talk with their friends.

Today there is chicken, rice, and green beans. There is also an orange and a glass of milk. I don't like green beans, but I eat them because they are good for me.

My friend Leo has a sandwich from home. His mom makes very good sandwiches. Sometimes he gives me half, and I give him my orange. After lunch, we go outside and play soccer for twenty minutes.

=== a1-the-new-neighbor | The New Neighbor | Short Fiction | A1
There is a new family on our street. They live in the white house next to us. They have a boy and a big black dog. The boy's name is Daniel.

On Sunday, my mom makes a cake. "Let's take this cake to our new neighbors," she says. We walk to the white house and knock on the door. Daniel's mother opens it. She is surprised and very happy.

Daniel and I play in the garden with his dog. He is from another city, and he doesn't have friends here. Now he has one friend, and that is me.

=== a1-my-favorite-food | My Favorite Food | Food | A1
My favorite food is soup. I like many kinds of soup, but tomato soup is the best. My dad makes it on cold days. He uses fresh tomatoes, onions, and a little garlic.

We eat the soup with warm bread. Sometimes we put cheese on top. It smells very good, and the kitchen is warm and happy.

My sister does not like soup. She likes pasta with red sauce. My mom likes fish and salad. We all like different things, but we always eat dinner together at the big table.

=== a1-a-day-at-the-beach | A Day at the Beach | Places | A1
In summer, my family goes to the beach. The beach is one hour from our town by car. We leave early in the morning, and we take sandwiches, water, and fruit.

The sand is hot, and the water is blue and cold. My dad and I swim in the sea. My mom reads a book under a big umbrella. My little sister builds a sand castle.

In the afternoon, we eat ice cream. Then we walk on the beach and look for shells. We go home in the evening. We are tired, but very happy.

=== a1-my-best-friend | My Best Friend | People | A1
My best friend is Rosa. She is eleven, like me. We are in the same class at school. Rosa has long black hair and big brown eyes. She is tall and very smart.

Rosa loves music. She plays the piano, and she sings very well. I can't sing, but I can dance. Sometimes she plays the piano, and I dance in her living room.

We talk every day. We walk to school together, and we eat lunch together. When I am sad, Rosa always makes me laugh. She is a great friend.

=== a1-the-lost-cat | The Lost Cat | Short Fiction | A1
Lily has a small white cat called Snow. One evening, Snow is not at home. Lily looks in the kitchen, in the bedroom, and in the garden. The cat is not there.

Lily is very sad. She makes a poster with a photo of Snow. She puts it on a tree in the street. Her dad helps her. They walk around the town and call, "Snow! Snow!"

The next morning, Lily hears a sound at the door. She opens it, and there is Snow. The cat is dirty and hungry, but she is fine. Lily gives her food and a big hug.

=== a1-weekend-plans | My Weekend | Hobbies | A1
I love the weekend. On Saturday, I don't go to school, so I sleep late. I get up at nine o'clock and eat pancakes with my family.

In the afternoon, I play basketball with my friends. We play in the park near my school. I am not very tall, but I am fast. After the game, we drink cold water and talk.

On Sunday, I visit my grandparents. My grandmother makes a big lunch, and my grandfather tells funny stories. In the evening, I read a book and go to bed early. The weekend is always too short.

=== a1-in-the-city | In the City | Places | A1
I live in a big city. There are tall buildings, busy streets, and many cars. There are also parks, museums, and lots of cafes. The city is never quiet.

Every day, I go to work by train. The train is fast, but it is often full. I listen to music and look out the window. I can see the river and the bridges.

On the weekend, I like to walk in the old part of the city. The streets there are small and pretty. I sit in a cafe, drink coffee, and watch the people.

=== a1-the-birthday-party | The Birthday Party | Family | A1
Today is my brother's birthday. He is seven. We have a party at our house in the afternoon. There are balloons in every room, and there is a big chocolate cake on the table.

Ten children come to the party. They play games in the garden and run around. My mom takes many photos. My dad plays music, and everybody dances.

At five o'clock, we sing "Happy Birthday." My brother blows out the seven candles on the cake. He opens his presents. His favorite present is a toy train. It is a very happy day.

=== a1-my-job | My Job | Work | A1
My name is Carlos, and I am a nurse. I work in a big hospital in the city. I start work at seven in the morning and finish at three in the afternoon.

I help sick people every day. I give them medicine, and I talk to them. Some people are scared, so I am always kind and calm. The doctors and nurses in my hospital are a good team.

My job is hard, and I am often tired. But I love it. When a patient smiles and says "thank you," I feel very happy.

=== a1-winter | Winter | Weather | A1
In my town, winter is very cold. It snows a lot, and the streets are white. The days are short, and the nights are long. The trees have no leaves.

I wear a warm coat, a hat, and gloves every day. My hands and my nose are always cold. At home, I drink hot tea and sit near the window.

But winter is also fun. On the weekend, my friends and I go to the hill near the school. We slide down on the snow and make a big snowman. Then we go home for hot soup.

=== a1-at-the-cafe | At the Cafe | Food | A1
There is a small cafe on my street. It is open every day from eight o'clock. The cafe has six tables and big windows. It is warm and bright inside.

I go there on Friday after work. I usually order a coffee with milk and a piece of apple cake. The waiter is a young man called Ben. He knows my name, and he always says hello.

Sometimes my friend Nina comes with me. She drinks green tea, and she eats a cookie. We sit by the window and talk for an hour. It is my favorite place in town.

=== a1-the-garden | Grandpa's Garden | Nature | A1
My grandpa has a big garden behind his house. There are flowers, vegetables, and three apple trees. He works in the garden every morning. He says it makes him happy and strong.

In spring, I help him. We plant carrots, potatoes, and beans. I water the plants with a green watering can. Grandpa shows me the small birds in the trees.

In autumn, we pick the apples. Some are red, and some are yellow. Grandma makes apple pie, and we eat it together in the kitchen. Food from the garden tastes the best.
