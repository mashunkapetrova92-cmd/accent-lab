=== a1-the-flower-shop | The Flower Shop | Work | A1
Julia has a small flower shop near the train station. The shop is full of color. There are roses, tulips, and sunflowers. There are also small plants for the home.

Julia opens the shop at eight o'clock. Many people buy flowers on their way to work. Some flowers are for birthdays, and some are for a mother or a friend. Some people buy one flower, and some buy a big bunch.

Julia loves her job. She says flowers make people happy. At the end of the day, she takes the old flowers home and puts them on her kitchen table.

=== a1-the-park-near-my-home | The Park | Places | A1
There is a big park near my home. It has green grass, tall trees, and a small lake. In the middle of the park, there is a playground for children.

In the morning, people run and walk their dogs in the park. At lunchtime, office workers sit on the grass and eat sandwiches. In the afternoon, children come from school and play.

I go to the park every evening after work. I walk around the lake two times. It takes about thirty minutes. Then I sit on a bench and watch the ducks. It is very relaxing.

=== a1-the-big-game | The Big Game | Hobbies | A1
Today my team plays in a big soccer game. We play against a team from another town. Many parents and friends come to watch. My mom and dad sit in the front.

The other team is very good. After the first half, they have one goal, and we have zero. Our coach says, "Don't stop. Work together." We go back on the field and try very hard.

In the second half, my friend Pedro scores a goal. Then, two minutes before the end, I score a goal. We win! Everybody shouts and jumps. It is the best day.

=== a1-a-new-friend | A New Student | School | A1
Today there is a new student in my class. Her name is Aiko, and she is from Japan. She is quiet, and she doesn't know anybody. She sits alone at lunch.

I go to her table and sit down. "Hi, I'm Sophie," I say. Aiko smiles. Her English is good. She tells me about her old school and her city. I tell her about our town.

After lunch, I show her the library and the gym. Now we sit together every day. Aiko teaches me some Japanese words, and I help her with math.

=== a1-the-birthday-present | The Present | Short Fiction | A1
It is Anna's birthday next week. Her brother Tom wants to buy her a present, but he has only five dollars. He goes to many shops, but everything is too expensive.

Then Tom has an idea. He takes some paper and colored pencils. He works on his present every evening in his room. He doesn't show it to anybody.

On Anna's birthday, Tom gives her a small book. It is full of his drawings and funny stories about them. Anna reads it and laughs. "This is the best present," she says, and she hugs him.

=== a1-breakfast | Breakfast | Food | A1
People eat many different things for breakfast. In my house, we all like different food. My dad eats eggs and toast. My mom has only coffee and a small piece of fruit.

My little brother eats cereal with milk every day. He never wants anything else. I like yogurt with fruit and honey. Sometimes I eat pancakes on the weekend.

My grandmother says breakfast is the most important meal of the day. She eats a big breakfast every morning at seven o'clock. She is eighty-two, and she is very healthy, so maybe she is right.

=== a1-the-horse | The White Horse | Animals | A1
There is a white horse in the field near my school. She is beautiful and very big. Every day, I stop at the fence and look at her. Sometimes she comes to the fence.

One day, a farmer is in the field. "Do you like my horse?" he asks. "Her name is Star." He gives me a carrot. I give it to Star, and she eats it from my hand. Her nose is very soft.

Now I bring a carrot or an apple every day. Star knows me. When she sees me, she runs to the fence. She is my friend.

=== a1-the-photo | The Old Photo | Family | A1
In my grandmother's house, there is an old photo on the wall. It is black and white. In the photo, there is a young man and a young woman. They are standing in front of a church.

"Who are these people?" I ask my grandmother. She laughs. "That is me, and that is your grandfather," she says. "It is our wedding day." I am very surprised. They look so young.

My grandmother tells me about the wedding. There were a hundred people and a big cake. In the evening, everybody danced. She smiles when she talks about it.

=== a1-the-bakery-line | Sunday Bread | Daily Life | A1
Every Sunday morning, my dad goes to the bakery. He leaves the house at eight o'clock, and he always takes me with him. We walk there together. It takes ten minutes.

There is always a line in front of the bakery. We wait and talk with our neighbors. The smell of fresh bread is wonderful. When we go inside, it is very warm.

My dad buys a big loaf of bread and some croissants. The baker gives me a small cookie. We walk home, and my mom makes coffee. Then we eat breakfast together.

=== a1-the-office | My Office | Work | A1
I work in an office in the city center. My office is on the tenth floor of a tall building. From my desk, I can see the river and the old bridge.

There are twelve people in my team. We work on computers all day. We write emails, answer the phone, and have many meetings. My boss is a woman called Mrs. Lopez. She is strict, but fair.

At lunch, I eat with my colleagues in the kitchen. We talk about our weekends and our families. I finish work at five o'clock and take the bus home.

=== a1-the-river | The River | Nature | A1
A big river goes through my town. It is long and wide, and the water is green. There are three bridges over it. The oldest bridge is made of stone.

In summer, people swim in the river and sit on the grass next to it. Some people go fishing, and some people go out in small boats. Children throw stones into the water.

In winter, the river is cold and gray. Sometimes there is ice on it. But I still like to walk next to it. The river is always beautiful, in every season.

=== a1-the-airport | At the Airport | Travel | A1
Today I am at the airport. I am going to visit my sister in another country. My flight is at eleven o'clock, so I am here at nine. The airport is big and very busy.

First, I give my big bag to a woman at the desk. Then I go through security. I take my phone and my keys out of my pockets. After that, I find my gate.

I have one hour, so I buy a coffee and a book. Then I sit by the big window and watch the planes. At half past ten, I get on the plane.

=== a1-the-clever-crow | The Clever Crow | Short Fiction | A1
It is a hot summer day, and a crow is very thirsty. He flies around and looks for water. Then he finds a tall jar in a garden. There is a little water in the bottom.

The crow puts his head into the jar, but he can't drink. His beak is too short. He thinks and thinks. Then he has an idea.

He picks up a small stone and drops it into the jar. Then another stone, and another. Slowly, the water comes up. Finally, the crow can drink. He is very happy, and very clever.

=== a1-a-day-off | A Day Off | Daily Life | A1
Today I don't go to work. It is my day off. I don't set my alarm, and I sleep until nine o'clock. The sun is shining through the window.

I make a big breakfast with eggs, toast, and orange juice. I eat it on my balcony. Then I call my friend Nina. We go for a long walk by the river and have lunch in a nice restaurant.

In the afternoon, I read a book on the sofa and take a short nap. In the evening, I cook a good dinner and watch a movie. It is a perfect day.

=== a1-the-rainbow | After the Rain | Weather | A1
It rains all morning. The sky is dark, and the wind is strong. We can't play outside, so my sister and I sit by the window and wait.

At two o'clock, the rain stops. The clouds move away, and the sun comes out. Then my sister shouts, "Look! A rainbow!" There is a big rainbow over the hills. It has many colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple.

We run outside to see it better. Our neighbors come out too. Everybody looks at the sky. After ten minutes, the rainbow goes away. But we still remember it.

=== a1-my-favorite-season | My Favorite Season | Weather | A1
My favorite season is summer. In summer, the days are long and hot. There is no school, and I have a lot of free time. I can stay outside until nine o'clock in the evening.

In summer, I go to the beach with my family. I swim in the sea and eat ice cream. I also ride my bike and play with my friends in the park every day.

My brother doesn't like summer. He says it is too hot. His favorite season is winter, because he loves snow. We are very different, but we are good brothers.

=== a1-the-painter | The Painter | Work | A1
Mr. Hill is a painter, but he doesn't paint pictures. He paints houses. He paints walls, doors, and windows. He works with his son, and they have a small white van.

This week, they are painting our house. The old color is brown. The new color is light yellow. They start at eight o'clock every morning and work all day. They listen to the radio and sing.

After four days, the house is finished. It looks new and bright. My mom is very happy. She makes coffee and cake for Mr. Hill and his son.

=== a1-the-concert | The Concert | Hobbies | A1
Tonight I am going to a concert with my best friend. The band is our favorite band. The concert is in a big hall in the city. It starts at eight o'clock.

We arrive at seven, and there are already many people. Everybody is excited. When the band comes on, everybody shouts. The music is very loud. We sing all the songs and dance.

The concert is two hours long. At the end, my ears are ringing, and my feet hurt. But I am very happy. We talk about the concert all the way home.

=== a1-the-hospital-visit | Visiting Grandpa | Health | A1
My grandfather is in the hospital. He has a problem with his heart, but the doctors say he is getting better. Today, my mom and I visit him.

We bring him flowers, grapes, and a newspaper. His room is clean and quiet. He is in bed, but he is happy to see us. He says the food in the hospital is terrible.

We stay for two hours. We talk, and I tell him about school. Before we go, he says, "Next week, I am going home." I hug him, and he smiles.

=== a1-good-friends | Two Friends | Short Fiction | A1
Jack is a big dog, and Pip is a small gray mouse. They live in the same old farmhouse. Jack sleeps by the fire, and Pip lives in a little hole in the wall.

One cold night, a cat comes into the house. The cat sees Pip and runs after her. Pip is very scared. She runs to Jack and hides under his big ear.

Jack opens his eyes and makes a loud sound. The cat is scared, and it runs away. Pip comes out and says, "Thank you, Jack." From that night, Jack and Pip are best friends.
