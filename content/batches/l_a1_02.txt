=== a1-the-fish-tank | The Fish Tank | Animals | A1
In my classroom, there is a big fish tank. There are eight fish in it. Some are orange, some are yellow, and one is blue. The blue fish is the biggest, and we call him King.

Every week, two students take care of the fish. They give them food in the morning and look at the water. This week, it is my job with my friend Omar.

The fish are beautiful. When we are tired, our teacher says, "Look at the fish for one minute." We watch them swim, and we feel calm again.

=== a1-a-cold-day-in-the-park | Feeding the Ducks | Nature | A1
There is a small lake in the park near my house. Many ducks live on the lake. On Sunday afternoons, my grandfather and I go there to see them.

We take some old bread and some seeds in a bag. When the ducks see us, they swim very fast to the side of the lake. They are noisy and hungry. The baby ducks are small and yellow.

My grandfather sits on a bench and watches. I give the food to the ducks, and they eat it all. Then we walk around the lake and go home for tea.

=== a1-my-house | My House | Home | A1
I live in a house with my parents and my brother. Our house has two floors. Downstairs, there is a living room, a kitchen, and a bathroom. Upstairs, there are three bedrooms.

The kitchen is my favorite room. It is big and sunny, and it has a round table. We eat all our meals there. My mom has many plants on the windows.

Behind the house, there is a garden with green grass and a tall tree. My dad makes a swing for us on the tree. In summer, we eat dinner in the garden.

=== a1-the-pizza-night | Pizza Night | Food | A1
Friday is pizza night in our family. We don't buy pizza. We make it at home. Everybody helps, and the kitchen is always a little dirty after.

My mom makes the dough in the afternoon. Then we put tomato sauce on it. My dad likes mushrooms and peppers on his pizza. My sister likes only cheese. I like pizza with ham and pineapple.

We cook the pizzas in the hot oven for fifteen minutes. Then we sit in the living room and eat them in front of a movie. It is the best night of the week.

=== a1-the-postman | The Mail Carrier | Work | A1
Every morning at ten o'clock, Mrs. Brown comes to our street. She is a mail carrier, and she brings letters and packages. She walks to every house with a big blue bag.

Mrs. Brown walks about fifteen kilometers every day. She works in the sun, in the rain, and in the snow. She says the job keeps her strong.

All the children on our street know her. She always says hello, and she knows the names of all the dogs. In December, we give her a card and a box of chocolates.

=== a1-the-little-boat | The Little Boat | Short Fiction | A1
Jack makes a little boat from paper. It is white with a small blue flag. He takes it to the river near his house and puts it on the water.

The boat sails slowly down the river. Jack walks next to it. The boat goes past a big rock and a tall tree. A duck swims next to it and looks at it.

Then the boat goes under a bridge. On the other side, a little girl sees it. She picks it up out of the water and smiles. Jack waves at her, and she waves back. Now they are friends.

=== a1-summer-holiday | A Summer Vacation | Travel | A1
In July, my family goes on vacation. This year, we go to a small town by a lake. We stay in a wooden house with a big garden. There are mountains all around the lake.

Every day, we swim in the lake. The water is cold, but it is clean and clear. In the afternoon, we walk in the forest or ride our bikes. My dad likes fishing.

In the evening, we cook dinner outside and eat in the garden. We look at the stars in the sky. There are many stars here, more than in the city. I love this place.

=== a1-my-uncle | My Uncle | Family | A1
My uncle Peter is my mom's brother. He is forty years old, and he is very tall. He has a black beard and a loud laugh. He lives on a farm with his wife and their three children.

On the farm, there are cows, sheep, chickens, and two horses. My uncle gets up at five o'clock every morning. He works outside all day. He is never tired.

Every summer, I stay with my uncle for two weeks. I help him with the animals, and I ride the horses. I love the farm, and I love my uncle.

=== a1-the-supermarket | At the Supermarket | Shopping | A1
On Thursday evenings, I go to the supermarket. I always have a list, because I forget things. Today I need milk, eggs, bread, rice, and some fruit.

The supermarket is big and bright. I take a cart and walk down the long rows. I find the milk and the eggs first. Then I look for the rice. The fruit is near the door. I buy apples and a melon.

At the end, I stand in line and pay. The woman at the checkout is friendly. She puts my things in two bags. I carry them to my car and drive home.

=== a1-a-good-night | Bedtime | Daily Life | A1
At nine o'clock, it is bedtime in our house. I put on my pajamas and brush my teeth. Then I go to my bedroom and get into bed.

My dad comes into my room and sits on my bed. Every night, he reads a story to me. Tonight the story is about a dragon and a little girl. It is exciting, and I don't want it to stop.

After the story, my dad turns off the light. "Good night," he says. "Sleep well." I close my eyes and think about the dragon. Soon I am asleep.

=== a1-the-farm | On the Farm | Animals | A1
Maria lives on a farm with her parents. There are many animals on the farm. There are cows, pigs, goats, and lots of chickens. There is also an old dog called Rex.

Every morning before school, Maria helps her mother. She gives food to the chickens and looks for eggs. Sometimes she finds ten eggs, and sometimes she finds twenty.

After school, Maria plays with the baby goats. They are small and funny, and they jump everywhere. In the evening, she sits on the steps with Rex and watches the sun go down.

=== a1-the-music-lesson | The Guitar Lesson | Hobbies | A1
Every Monday, I have a guitar lesson. My teacher is an old man called Mr. Stone. He has long gray hair, and he plays the guitar very well.

The lesson is one hour long. First, I play some easy songs. Then Mr. Stone shows me something new. Today, I learn a new song. It is hard, and my fingers hurt a little.

At home, I practice every day for thirty minutes. My family listens to me. My little sister sings with my music. Next month, I play in a small concert at my school.

=== a1-the-umbrella | The Yellow Umbrella | Short Fiction | A1
Kim has a yellow umbrella. It is big and bright, and she loves it. One morning, it is raining, and she takes it to the bus stop.

At the bus stop, there is an old man with no umbrella. He is wet and cold. Kim stands next to him and holds her umbrella over his head too. "Thank you," says the man.

The next week, Kim sees the old man again. He has a present for her. It is a small yellow raincoat. "Now you can be dry too," he says. Kim laughs and puts it on.

=== a1-my-phone | My Phone | Technology | A1
I have a phone, and I use it every day. I call my parents, and I send messages to my friends. I also take photos with it. My phone has a good camera.

In the morning, my phone wakes me up at seven o'clock. On the bus, I listen to music and read the news. At work, I check my calendar on it.

But in the evening, I put my phone in the kitchen. I don't take it to my bedroom. I read a book before bed, and I sleep much better.

=== a1-the-swimming-pool | The Swimming Pool | Hobbies | A1
There is a big swimming pool in my town. It is open every day from seven in the morning to nine at night. I go there with my friend Lucas on Saturdays.

The water is warm, and the pool is very clean. We swim for one hour. Lucas is a good swimmer, and he is very fast. I am slow, but I can swim for a long time.

After swimming, we are always very hungry. We go to the cafe next to the pool and eat hot fries. Then we take the bus home. We are tired, but we feel great.

=== a1-the-new-baby | The New Baby | Family | A1
We have a new baby in our family. She is my little sister, and her name is Grace. She is two weeks old. She is very small, and she has a lot of black hair.

Grace sleeps a lot. When she is awake, she cries, and she is hungry. My mom and dad are always tired. I help them. I bring the clean clothes, and I sing to the baby.

When I sing, Grace stops crying. She looks at me with her big dark eyes. My mom says Grace loves my songs. I think I am a good big brother.

=== a1-autumn | Autumn | Weather | A1
In autumn, the weather is cool and windy. The days are shorter, and it often rains. The leaves on the trees are red, orange, and yellow. Then they fall to the ground.

I like walking in the park in autumn. The leaves make a sound under my feet. My little dog runs in the leaves and plays. The air smells fresh and clean.

In autumn, we eat warm food. My mom makes pumpkin soup and apple cake. In the evening, we sit together in the living room and drink hot tea. Autumn is cozy.

=== a1-the-old-clock | The Old Clock | Short Fiction | A1
In my grandfather's house, there is a very old clock. It is tall and brown, and it stands in the hall. Every hour, it makes a loud sound. Bong, bong, bong!

One day, the clock stops. My grandfather is very sad. "This clock was my father's clock," he says. He tries to fix it, but he can't.

The next day, we take the clock to a small shop in town. An old woman looks at it for a long time. Then she opens it and fixes a small part. Bong! The clock works again, and my grandfather smiles.

=== a1-the-baker | The Baker | Work | A1
My aunt Sophie is a baker. She has a small bakery in the center of our town. She starts work at four o'clock in the morning, when everybody is asleep.

She makes bread, cakes, and cookies. Her bakery smells wonderful. People come every morning to buy fresh bread for breakfast. Her chocolate cake is very popular.

My aunt finishes work at two o'clock in the afternoon. Then she goes home and sleeps for two hours. She says her job is hard, but she loves it. I want to work in her bakery one day.

=== a1-a-walk-in-the-forest | A Walk in the Forest | Nature | A1
On Sunday, my family takes a walk in the forest. The forest is near our town. It is big and green, and it is very quiet. We walk on a small path between the tall trees.

We see many things. There are birds in the trees and small flowers on the ground. My brother finds a big beetle. Then we see a deer. It looks at us and runs away.

After two hours, we stop near a small river. We sit on the rocks and eat our sandwiches. The water is very cold. Then we walk back home.
