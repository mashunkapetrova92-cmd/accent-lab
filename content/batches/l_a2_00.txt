=== a2-my-first-job | My First Job | Work | A2
When I was sixteen, I got my first job. I worked in a small cafe near my school on Saturdays and Sundays. I washed the dishes, cleaned the tables, and sometimes made coffee for the customers.

The first day was terrible. I dropped three cups, and I forgot two orders. I went home and cried. I wanted to stop, but my mother said, "Go back next weekend. It will be easier." She was right.

After a few weeks, I knew all the regular customers and their favorite drinks. An old man came every Sunday at ten o'clock and always ordered black coffee and a piece of lemon cake. He always left a big tip and told me jokes.

I worked at that cafe for two years. The money wasn't very good, but I learned a lot. I learned to work hard, to be polite, and to stay calm when it was busy. Those lessons are still useful today.

=== a2-a-trip-to-the-mountains | A Weekend in the Mountains | Travel | A2
Last month, my friends and I went to the mountains for the weekend. We left early on Saturday morning and drove for four hours. The roads were narrow and full of curves, and my friend Tom felt sick.

We stayed in a small wooden house near a lake. It didn't have a television or the internet, but it had a big fireplace and a beautiful view. On the first evening, we cooked dinner together and played cards until midnight.

On Sunday, we climbed to the top of a mountain. It was harder than we expected. The path was steep and rocky, and it took us three hours. But the view from the top was amazing. We could see lakes, forests, and small villages far below us.

We were very tired when we got home on Sunday night, but everybody agreed it was a great trip. We are going to go back in the winter, when there is snow.

=== a2-the-lost-wallet | The Lost Wallet | Short Fiction | A2
One evening, Daniel was walking home from work when he saw a brown wallet on the sidewalk. He picked it up and looked inside. There was a lot of money, two bank cards, and a driver's license with a woman's photo.

Daniel thought about the money for a moment. He needed a new bike, and nobody saw him. But then he looked at the photo again. The woman looked kind, and she probably needed her cards.

The address was on the license, so Daniel walked there. It was a small yellow house on the other side of the park. An old woman opened the door. When she saw the wallet, she started to cry. "My husband's pension is in there," she said. "Thank you so much."

The next week, Daniel received a letter. Inside, there was a thank you card and a small cake. He didn't get any money, but he felt happier than he expected.

=== a2-healthy-habits | Healthy Habits | Health | A2
Many people want to be healthier, but they don't know where to start. Doctors say that small changes are often better than big ones. You don't need to change your whole life in one day.

First, try to drink more water. Many people drink coffee or soda all day and forget about water. A big glass of water in the morning is a good start. Second, walk more. You can get off the bus one stop early or take the stairs instead of the elevator.

Sleep is also very important. Adults need seven to nine hours of sleep every night. If you go to bed at the same time every day, it is easier to fall asleep. It is also a good idea to put your phone away an hour before bed.

Finally, eat more fruit and vegetables. They are cheaper than many people think, and they make you feel better. Healthy habits take time, but they are worth it.

=== a2-my-hometown | My Hometown | Places | A2
I grew up in a small town by the sea. About five thousand people live there. When I was a child, I thought it was the most boring place in the world. Now I think it is one of the most beautiful.

The town has one main street with a few shops, two cafes, and a post office. At the end of the street, there is a long beach with white sand. In summer, the town is full of tourists, and it is noisy and busy. In winter, it is very quiet, and the wind is cold.

My parents still live there, in the same house. I visit them three or four times a year. When I arrive, I always walk on the beach first. The smell of the sea makes me feel like a child again.

I live in a big city now, and I like my life here. But my small town will always be home.

=== a2-the-best-holiday | The Best Vacation Ever | Travel | A2
Three years ago, I went to Italy with my sister. It was our first trip abroad together, and it was the best vacation of my life. We spent two weeks there and visited four cities.

We started in Rome. We saw the famous old buildings and threw coins into a fountain. Then we took a train to Florence, where we visited museums and ate the best ice cream I have ever tasted. After that, we went to Venice. There are no cars there, only boats, and the streets are full of water.

Our last stop was a small village by the sea. We stayed with a family, and the grandmother cooked dinner for us every night. She didn't speak English, and we didn't speak Italian, but we laughed a lot.

When we came home, we were sad. Now we are saving money, because next year we want to go to Spain.

=== a2-a-busy-day-at-the-hospital | A Day in the Life of a Nurse | Work | A2
Maria works as a nurse in a children's hospital. Her day usually starts at seven in the morning, but she gets up at half past five. She takes a bus and a train to get to work.

When she arrives, she talks to the night nurses. They tell her about the children and any problems during the night. Then she visits every child on her floor. She checks their temperature, gives them medicine, and talks to their parents.

Some days are very difficult. Some children are very sick, and their parents are worried and scared. Maria tries to be calm and kind. She often plays games with the children or reads them stories, because it helps them forget the hospital for a while.

Maria finishes work at seven in the evening. She is always tired, but she says her job is the most important thing in her life. She can't imagine doing anything else.

=== a2-shopping-online | Shopping Online | Technology | A2
Ten years ago, most people went to shops to buy clothes, books, and electronics. Today, many people buy almost everything online. You can order food, furniture, and even a car from your sofa.

Online shopping has many advantages. It is fast and easy, and you can do it at any time of the day or night. You can compare prices from many shops in a few minutes. Things are often cheaper online, and they come to your door.

But there are some problems too. You can't touch or try on the things before you buy them. Sometimes clothes are the wrong size, and you have to send them back. Some people also miss the experience of walking around a market or a bookstore and talking to people.

My grandmother still prefers real shops. She says, "I want to see the tomatoes before I buy them." I think she has a good point.

=== a2-learning-to-cook | Learning to Cook | Food | A2
When I moved into my first apartment, I couldn't cook at all. For the first month, I ate pizza, sandwiches, and instant noodles every day. I felt tired all the time, and I spent too much money.

So I decided to learn. I called my mother, and she gave me some easy recipes. I started with simple things, like scrambled eggs and pasta with tomato sauce. Then I tried soup and rice dishes. Some of my first meals were terrible. Once, I burned the rice so badly that the whole apartment smelled for three days.

Slowly, I got better. I watched cooking videos and bought a good knife. I learned to use fresh vegetables and herbs. Now I cook dinner almost every evening, and I often invite friends.

Cooking is now one of my favorite hobbies. It helps me relax after work, and it is much healthier than fast food.

=== a2-the-wrong-train | The Wrong Train | Short Fiction | A2
Last summer, Emma was traveling alone for the first time. She wanted to visit her aunt in a city about two hours away. She was nervous, but she had her ticket and a map.

At the station, it was very busy. She saw a train on platform four and got on quickly. She found a seat by the window and started to read her book. After an hour, she looked out of the window. There were fields and cows, but no city. Something was wrong.

Emma asked the woman next to her. "This train goes to the coast," the woman said. Emma's face went red. She was on the wrong train! At the next station, she got off and called her aunt.

Her aunt laughed. "Don't worry," she said. "I'll come and get you." The next day, they went to the coast together, because Emma said it looked beautiful from the train.

=== a2-my-grandfathers-stories | My Grandfather's Stories | Family | A2
My grandfather was born in a small village eighty years ago. His family was poor, and he started working on a farm when he was only twelve years old. He didn't go to school for very long, but he was very clever.

When I was a child, I spent every summer at his house. In the evenings, we sat outside and he told me stories about his life. He told me about the cold winters, when the snow was higher than the windows. He told me about the day he met my grandmother at a dance.

My favorite story was about his first trip to the city. He was nineteen, and he had never seen a tall building or a bus before. He got lost and walked around for hours until a police officer helped him.

My grandfather died two years ago. I miss him a lot, but I still remember all his stories, and now I tell them to my children.

=== a2-the-weather-forecast | Watching the Weather | Weather | A2
Every evening, my father watches the weather forecast on television. He says it is the most important part of the news. He is a farmer, so the weather is very important for his work.

When the forecast says it will rain, he is happy in spring, because the plants need water. But in late summer, rain can be a problem, because it is time to collect the wheat. If it rains too much, the wheat gets wet and bad.

Weather forecasts are much better than they were in the past. Scientists use satellites and powerful computers to predict the weather. Today, a forecast for the next three days is usually correct. But a forecast for two weeks is still a guess.

My father doesn't always trust the television. He also looks at the sky, the clouds, and the birds. He says the birds fly lower before it rains. Sometimes he is right, and the television is wrong.

=== a2-a-new-language | Learning a New Language | School | A2
Last year, I started to learn Spanish. I wanted to travel to South America, and I wanted to talk to people there. I joined an evening class at a language school near my office.

At first, it was very difficult. The grammar was confusing, and I couldn't remember new words. In the first lesson, the teacher spoke only Spanish, and I understood almost nothing. I felt stupid and wanted to give up.

But I didn't. I studied for twenty minutes every day. I used an app on my phone, and I watched Spanish movies with subtitles. I also found a conversation partner online. Her name is Lucia, and she lives in Mexico. Every Sunday, we talk for an hour, half in Spanish and half in English.

Now, after one year, I can have simple conversations. I still make a lot of mistakes, but I am not afraid to speak. Next summer, I am going to Peru.

=== a2-the-school-trip | The School Trip | School | A2
In May, our class went on a school trip to the capital city. We traveled by bus, and the trip took five hours. Everybody was excited, and nobody slept on the bus.

On the first day, we visited the national museum. We saw old paintings, ancient coins, and the clothes of kings and queens. Our guide was very funny, and she told us strange stories about the past. In the evening, we stayed in a youth hostel. I shared a room with three friends, and we talked until two in the morning.

The next day, we went on a boat trip on the river and saw the most famous buildings in the city. Then we had free time for shopping. I bought some postcards and a small present for my mother.

We came home on the third day. We were tired, but it was the best school trip ever. I took more than two hundred photos.

=== a2-the-stray-dog | The Dog at the Door | Animals | A2
One rainy night in November, my family heard a noise at the front door. My father opened it, and there was a small brown dog. It was wet, thin, and very scared. It didn't have a collar.

We brought the dog inside and gave it some food and water. It ate everything very quickly. Then it lay down in front of the heater and slept. My little brother wanted to keep it, but my mother said, "Maybe it has a family. We have to look for them."

The next day, we took the dog to the vet. The vet checked it, but there was no chip. We put posters on the trees in our street and wrote a message online. Nobody called.

After two weeks, my mother said, "OK, the dog can stay." My brother was so happy that he jumped up and down. We called the dog Lucky, because it was lucky to find us. Now Lucky is part of our family.

=== a2-moving-to-a-new-city | Moving to a New City | Daily Life | A2
Six months ago, I moved to a new city for work. I didn't know anybody here, and the first weeks were very lonely. I went to work, came home, and watched television. On the weekends, I didn't talk to anybody.

Then I decided to change things. I joined a running club that meets in the park on Tuesday evenings. The people were very friendly, and after the run, we often went to a cafe. I also started a pottery class on Saturdays.

Slowly, I made some friends. Now I have a group of people I see every week. Last month, one of them invited me to her birthday party, and I met even more people there.

Moving to a new place is hard, especially when you are alone. My advice is to say yes to everything. Go to every party, join a club, and talk to your neighbors. It is scary at first, but it really helps.

=== a2-the-perfect-pancakes | Pancakes on Sunday | Food | A2
In my family, Sunday breakfast is always pancakes. My father started this tradition when I was a little girl, and we still do it today. Even now, when I visit my parents, we make pancakes together.

The recipe is very simple. You need flour, milk, eggs, a little sugar, and a little salt. You mix everything in a big bowl until it is smooth. Then you heat a pan with a little butter and pour in some of the mixture. When you see small bubbles on top, you turn the pancake over.

The first pancake is never perfect. My father always says, "The first one is for the cook," and he eats it himself. The rest are golden and soft.

We eat them with different things. My mother likes lemon and sugar. My brother puts chocolate on everything. I prefer fresh berries and honey. It is a simple meal, but it is full of happy memories.

=== a2-a-famous-scientist | Marie Curie | History | A2
Marie Curie was one of the most famous scientists in history. She was born in Poland in eighteen sixty-seven. At that time, women could not study at the university in Poland, so she moved to Paris when she was twenty-four.

In Paris, she studied physics and mathematics. She was very poor, and her small room was very cold in winter. Sometimes she forgot to eat because she was studying. She met another scientist, Pierre Curie, and they got married.

Together, Marie and Pierre studied radioactivity. They discovered two new elements. In nineteen oh three, they won the Nobel Prize in Physics. Marie was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize. Later, she won a second Nobel Prize, in chemistry.

Marie Curie worked very hard all her life. Her work helped doctors and scientists, and it is still important today. She showed the world that women can be great scientists.

=== a2-the-surprise-party | The Surprise Party | Short Fiction | A2
Last Friday was my best friend Jake's thirtieth birthday. His wife, Anna, wanted to have a surprise party for him. She invited twenty people and asked me to help.

The plan was simple. I had to take Jake to the movies in the evening. Meanwhile, Anna and the guests decorated the apartment and prepared the food. At nine o'clock, I had to bring Jake back home.

But there was a problem. In the middle of the movie, Jake said, "I'm tired. Let's go home." I didn't know what to do. I told him I wanted to see the end, but he didn't want to stay. So we left early.

I sent Anna a quick message from the car. When we got to the apartment, everything was dark and quiet. Jake opened the door, and everybody shouted, "Surprise!" Jake was so shocked that he dropped his keys. Then he laughed and said, "I knew something was strange about you tonight."

=== a2-the-public-library | Why I Love the Library | Places | A2
When I was a child, my mother took me to the public library every Saturday. I could borrow five books each week. I read all of them before the next Saturday. The library was my favorite place in the world.

Today, I still go to the library, but for different reasons. Of course, I still borrow books. But the library also has free internet, newspapers from many countries, and quiet rooms where I can work. When my apartment is too noisy, I go there to concentrate.

Libraries also offer many free activities. My local library has a book club, English classes for new immigrants, and computer lessons for older people. On Saturday mornings, there is a story hour for small children.

Some people think libraries are not necessary anymore, because everything is on the internet. I don't agree. A library is a place for everybody, rich or poor, young or old. It is free, warm, and open to all.
