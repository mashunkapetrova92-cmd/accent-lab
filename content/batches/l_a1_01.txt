=== a1-the-bus-driver | The Bus Driver | Work | A1
Mr. Lee is a bus driver. He drives the number ten bus in our town. He starts work very early, at six o'clock in the morning. He drives from the train station to the hospital and back again.

Mr. Lee knows many of the people on his bus. He says good morning to everybody. He helps old people with their bags, and he waits for children who are late.

At lunchtime, Mr. Lee stops at a small park. He eats a sandwich and feeds the birds. Then he drives again until four o'clock. He likes his job and his town.

=== a1-a-picnic-in-the-park | A Picnic in the Park | Hobbies | A1
It is a sunny Sunday, and my friends and I have a picnic in the park. We meet at eleven o'clock under a big tree. Everybody brings some food.

Emma brings sandwiches and a bottle of orange juice. Jack brings chips and grapes. I bring a chocolate cake. We put a big blanket on the grass and sit down. The food is very good.

After lunch, we play a game with a ball. Then we lie on the blanket and look at the clouds. One cloud looks like a rabbit. We stay in the park all afternoon.

=== a1-my-grandmother | My Grandmother | Family | A1
My grandmother is seventy-five years old. She has short white hair and blue eyes. She wears glasses, and she always smiles. She lives in a small house near the river.

Every day, my grandmother walks for one hour. Then she reads the newspaper and drinks coffee. She loves cooking. Her cookies are the best in the world.

I visit her every Wednesday after school. We sit in her kitchen and talk. She tells me stories about her life when she was young. I love my grandmother very much.

=== a1-new-shoes | New Shoes | Shopping | A1
My old shoes are too small, so today my mom and I go to the shoe store. The store is in the shopping center. It is big, and there are hundreds of shoes.

I try on black shoes, but they are not comfortable. Then I try on white sports shoes. They are soft and light, and I can walk and run in them. I love them.

The shoes are not cheap, but my mom says yes. I wear them out of the store. On the way home, I look at my feet all the time. They look great.

=== a1-the-zoo | A Trip to the Zoo | Animals | A1
Today my class goes to the zoo. We go by bus with our teacher. The zoo is big, and there are animals from all over the world.

First, we see the lions. They sleep in the sun. Then we see the monkeys. They are funny, and they jump from tree to tree. The children laugh at them. The giraffes are very tall, and they eat leaves from the top of the trees.

My favorite animals are the penguins. They are small and black and white. They swim very fast in the cold water. I want to come back to the zoo with my family.

=== a1-a-letter-to-a-friend | A Letter to a Friend | People | A1
Dear Paul,
How are you? I am fine. I am in a new city now, and I have a new school. My school is big, and my teacher is nice. I have two new friends. Their names are Mark and Julia.

Our new apartment is on the fifth floor. From my window, I can see the park and the river. My room is small, but it is bright.

I miss you and our old street. Do you still play soccer every Friday? Please write to me soon and tell me your news.
Your friend,
Mike

=== a1-cooking-with-dad | Cooking with Dad | Food | A1
Every Friday evening, my dad and I cook dinner together. My mom works late on Fridays, so it is our job. We look in the fridge and choose something to make.

Tonight, we make pasta with vegetables. I wash the tomatoes and the peppers. My dad cuts the onions because they make me cry. We cook the pasta in a big pot of water.

At seven o'clock, my mom comes home. The table is ready, and dinner is hot. She tries the pasta and says, "This is delicious!" My dad and I are very proud.

=== a1-the-library | The Library | Places | A1
There is a library near my house. It is a big old building with tall windows. Inside, it is very quiet. People read, study, and use the computers.

I go to the library every Tuesday. I can take five books home for three weeks. I like books about animals and stories about other countries. The librarian helps me find good books.

There is also a room for children. On Saturday mornings, a woman reads stories to the little kids. My little brother goes there with my mom. He loves the stories and the pictures.

=== a1-my-teacher | My Teacher | School | A1
My English teacher is Ms. Green. She is young, and she has curly red hair. She is very kind, and she is never angry. All the students like her.

In her lessons, we sing songs and play games. We read short stories and talk about them. Sometimes we watch funny videos in English. Her lessons are never boring.

When I make a mistake, Ms. Green helps me. She says, "That's OK. Try again." Now English is my favorite subject. I want to be a teacher like Ms. Green one day.

=== a1-the-magic-hat | The Magic Hat | Short Fiction | A1
Ella finds an old hat in her grandmother's house. It is black and tall, and it has a red ribbon. Ella puts the hat on her head and looks in the mirror.

Suddenly, a small white rabbit jumps out of the hat. Ella is very surprised. She takes off the hat and looks inside. There is nothing there. She puts it on again, and now there are flowers in her hands.

Ella runs to the kitchen. "Grandma, this hat is magic!" she says. Her grandmother laughs. "Yes," she says. "It was my hat when I was a girl. Now it is yours."

=== a1-spring | Spring | Weather | A1
I love spring. After the long, cold winter, the days are warm again. The sun is bright, and the sky is blue. There are new green leaves on the trees.

In spring, there are flowers everywhere. In my garden, there are red, yellow, and purple flowers. Birds sing in the morning, and I can hear them from my bed.

On spring days, I walk to school, and I don't need a big coat. After school, I ride my bike with my friends. We stay outside until dinner time. Spring is the best season.

=== a1-a-busy-mom | A Busy Mom | Family | A1
My mom is always busy. She gets up at six o'clock and makes breakfast for us. Then she drives my sister and me to school. After that, she goes to work at an office.

In the evening, my mom cooks dinner and helps us with our homework. She also cleans the house and washes our clothes. I help her a little. I wash the dishes and take out the trash.

On Sunday, my mom rests. She sits in the garden and reads a magazine. We bring her tea and cake. She says Sunday is her favorite day.

=== a1-the-train-trip | The Train Trip | Travel | A1
Today we go to the mountains by train. The trip is three hours long. We have big bags, sandwiches, and water. The train is clean and comfortable.

I sit by the window. First, I see the city and the tall buildings. Then I see fields, cows, and small villages. After two hours, there are big mountains with snow on the top.

My little brother sleeps, and my dad reads a book. My mom and I play cards. When the train stops, we get off and breathe the cold, fresh air. It is beautiful here.

=== a1-my-computer | My Computer | Technology | A1
I have a computer at home. It is on my desk in my bedroom. I use it every day for my homework. I write my stories on it, and I find information for school.

I also use my computer to talk to my cousins. They live in another country. We talk on video every Sunday. I can see their faces, and they can see mine.

Sometimes I play games on my computer, but only for one hour. My parents say too much time on the computer is bad for my eyes. They are right, so I go outside too.

=== a1-the-hungry-bear | The Hungry Bear | Short Fiction | A1
In a big forest, there is a brown bear. It is spring, and the bear is very hungry. He walks through the trees and looks for food.

He finds some berries, but they are not ready. He finds a river, but he can't catch a fish. Then he smells something sweet. It is honey in a tall tree. The bees are angry, and they fly around the bear.

The bear climbs the tree and takes the honey. The bees sting his nose, but he doesn't care. The honey is delicious. Now the bear is happy, and he goes to sleep.

=== a1-my-street | My Street | Places | A1
I live on a quiet street in a small town. There are twelve houses on my street. Most of the houses are white, and they have small gardens. There are tall trees on both sides.

At the end of my street, there is a bakery. It opens at seven o'clock, and the street smells of fresh bread. Next to the bakery, there is a small shop with fruit and vegetables.

The people on my street are friendly. In summer, we have a party in the street. Everybody brings food, and the children play together until late.

=== a1-sports-day | Sports Day | School | A1
Today is sports day at my school. There are no lessons, and all the students are outside. The weather is sunny and warm. Our parents come to watch us.

There are many races. I run in the hundred meter race, and I come third. My friend Ana wins the long jump. Our class also plays in a big game of tug of war. We pull very hard, and we win.

At the end of the day, the teachers give us medals. I am tired and hot, but I am very happy. I love sports day.

=== a1-the-doctor | At the Doctor's | Health | A1
Today I am sick. I have a headache and a sore throat, and I am very hot. I don't go to school. My mom takes me to the doctor.

The doctor is a tall woman with a friendly face. She looks in my mouth and listens to my heart. "You have a cold," she says. "Drink a lot of water, and stay in bed for two days."

At home, I lie in bed and sleep. My mom makes me chicken soup and honey tea. After two days, I feel much better. I am ready for school again.

=== a1-my-hobby | My Hobby | Hobbies | A1
My hobby is drawing. I draw every day after school. I have a big book of white paper and many colored pencils. I draw animals, people, and houses.

My favorite thing to draw is horses. I like their long legs and their beautiful faces. Sometimes I draw my cat, but she doesn't sit still. She always walks away.

My drawings are on the walls of my room. My aunt is an artist, and she gives me lessons on Saturdays. She says my drawings are very good. One day, I want to be an artist too.

=== a1-the-snowman | The Snowman | Short Fiction | A1
It snows all night, and in the morning, the garden is white. Ben and his sister Amy put on their coats and boots. They run outside and make a big snowman.

The snowman has two black stones for eyes and a carrot for a nose. Amy gives him her old red scarf. Ben puts his dad's hat on the snowman's head. "He is perfect," says Amy.

That night, Ben looks out of the window. The snowman is still there in the moonlight. Ben waves at him. In the morning, the sun is warm, and the snowman is very small. Ben is a little sad.
