=== a2-the-old-bookshop | The Old Bookstore | Places | A2
On a narrow street in the old part of town, there is a small bookstore. It is more than a hundred years old, and it belongs to an old man called Mr. Gray. His father and his grandfather owned it before him.

The store is dark and a little dusty. There are books everywhere, on the shelves, on the tables, and even on the floor. Some books are new, but most of them are old and secondhand. There is a strange, sweet smell of old paper. There is no music and no coffee machine, only a big gray cat who sleeps in the window.

Mr. Gray knows every book in the store. If you tell him what you like, he will always find something perfect for you. Last winter, he gave me an old book of poems. "You will love this," he said. He was right.

Many small bookstores closed in the last twenty years, because people buy books online. But Mr. Gray's store is still open. He says that people need a place where they can get lost in books.

=== a2-my-new-bike | Riding to Work | Daily Life | A2
Last spring, I decided to ride my bike to work instead of driving. My office is eight kilometers from my home. At first, my friends thought I was crazy. "What about the rain?" they asked. "What about the hills?"

The first week was hard. My legs hurt, and I arrived at work hot and tired. I had to take a change of clothes in my bag. But after two weeks, it got easier. After a month, I didn't want to drive anymore.

Riding to work has changed my life. I feel healthier and stronger, and I sleep better at night. I save a lot of money on gas and parking. And I am never stuck in traffic. In the morning, I ride through the park, and I see the sun come up over the lake. It is the best part of my day.

Of course, I don't ride in heavy snow or storms. On those days, I take the bus. But I ride almost every day, and I am much happier.

=== a2-the-talent-show | The Talent Show | Short Fiction | A2
Every year, Mia's school had a talent show. Students sang, danced, played music, and told jokes. Mia loved watching it, but she was too shy to be in it. She could play the violin very well, but she only played at home.

This year, her music teacher said, "Mia, you have to play in the show. You are really good." Mia said no at first. But her teacher didn't stop asking, and finally Mia agreed. For three weeks, she practiced every day after school.

On the night of the show, the hall was full. Mia's hands were cold, and her heart was beating fast. When she walked onto the stage, she saw hundreds of faces. She closed her eyes and started to play.

When she finished, the hall was silent for a second. Then everybody stood up and clapped. Mia opened her eyes and saw her parents. Her mother was crying. Mia didn't win the competition, but she didn't care. She wasn't shy anymore.

=== a2-a-strange-sandwich | Food Around the World | Food | A2
When people travel, they often try new and unusual food. Some of it is delicious, and some of it is very strange. Every country has its own special dishes.

In Japan, people eat a lot of raw fish. Sushi is rice with raw fish or vegetables, and it is now popular all over the world. In Mexico, people often eat tacos. These are soft corn tortillas with meat, beans, cheese, and spicy sauce. In India, many families eat rice and curry every day. Indian food uses many spices, like ginger, garlic, and cumin.

Some dishes surprise visitors. In some countries, people eat insects, like crickets and ants. They are full of protein, and some people say they taste like nuts. In Scotland, people eat haggis, which is made from the heart and other parts of a sheep.

I always try the local food when I travel. Sometimes I don't like it, but it is always interesting. Food tells you a lot about a culture.

=== a2-the-first-snow | The First Snow | Weather | A2
I grew up in a hot country, and I didn't see snow until I was twenty-five. That year, I moved to Canada to study at a university. I arrived in September, when the weather was still warm and sunny.

In November, the weather changed. It got colder every week, and the days got shorter. My friends told me to buy a big winter coat and good boots. I didn't believe them. "How cold can it be?" I said.

One morning in December, I woke up and the room was very bright. I looked out of the window, and everything was white. The trees, the cars, and the streets were all covered in snow. I ran outside in my pajamas and touched it. It was so cold and soft. I laughed like a child.

That winter was very hard for me. Sometimes it was minus twenty-five degrees. But I will never forget that first morning of snow.

=== a2-my-sister-the-doctor | My Sister, the Doctor | Family | A2
My older sister, Hannah, always wanted to be a doctor. When she was a little girl, she played doctor with her dolls. She put bandages on them and gave them pretend medicine.

At school, she was always the best student in her class. She studied very hard, especially science. After high school, she went to medical school. It took her many years, and it was very difficult. She often studied until two or three in the morning, and she didn't have much free time.

Now Hannah is thirty-two, and she works in a big hospital in the city. She is a children's doctor. She works long hours, and sometimes she works at night or on the weekend. When she comes to our parents' house for dinner, she often falls asleep on the sofa.

I am very proud of my sister. She is smart, kind, and very hardworking. She says the best part of her job is when a sick child gets better and goes home.

=== a2-the-farmers-market | The Farmers' Market | Shopping | A2
Every Saturday morning, there is a farmers' market in the main square of our town. Farmers from the villages around the town bring their fruit, vegetables, cheese, eggs, and bread. The market starts at seven o'clock and finishes at one.

I always go early, at about eight, because the best things sell quickly. I take two big bags and walk around slowly. I like to talk to the farmers. They tell me which vegetables are the freshest and how to cook them.

The food at the market is a little more expensive than in the supermarket. But it is fresher, and it tastes much better. The tomatoes taste like real tomatoes, and the strawberries are sweet and juicy. I also like knowing where my food comes from.

After shopping, I usually have a coffee at a small cafe next to the square. I sit outside and watch the people. For me, it is the perfect way to start the weekend.

=== a2-the-big-storm | The Big Storm | Weather | A2
Last October, there was a big storm in our town. The weather forecast said it was going to be bad, so most people stayed at home. The schools closed, and many shops didn't open.

The storm started in the afternoon. First, the wind got stronger and stronger. Then the rain came. It was so heavy that I couldn't see the houses across the street. At about six o'clock, the electricity went off. We lit candles and sat together in the living room. My little sister was scared, so my father told funny stories.

The wind was very loud all night. We heard a crash in the garden, but we didn't go outside. In the morning, the storm was over. A big tree was lying across our garden. It broke the fence, but luckily it didn't hit the house.

All the neighbors came out to help each other. We cleaned the streets together. It was a terrible storm, but it brought our street closer together.

=== a2-the-language-exchange | The Language Exchange | People | A2
Last year, I wanted to improve my French, but I didn't have money for lessons. Then a friend told me about a language exchange. In a language exchange, two people help each other learn their languages for free.

I found a partner on a website. His name was Lucas, and he was a student from Lyon. He wanted to practice his English. We decided to meet online every Thursday evening. For thirty minutes we spoke French, and then for thirty minutes we spoke English.

At first, I was nervous, and I made a lot of mistakes. But Lucas was very patient. He corrected me in a friendly way, and he taught me words that young people really use. I helped him with his pronunciation, because he found English sounds very difficult.

After six months, my French was much better. Last summer, I visited Lyon, and Lucas showed me his city. Now we are good friends, and we still talk every week.

=== a2-the-baby-bird | The Baby Bird | Short Fiction | A2
One morning in May, Sam was walking to school when he heard a small sound under a tree. He looked down and saw a tiny bird on the grass. It didn't have many feathers, and it couldn't fly. It was crying for its mother.

Sam looked up into the tree. There was a nest on a high branch, but it was too high for him. He didn't want to leave the bird alone, because there were many cats on his street. So he carefully picked it up and put it in his hat.

He was late for school, and his teacher was not happy. But when Sam showed her the bird, she smiled. She called a man who helped injured animals. He came to the school at lunchtime and took the bird.

Three weeks later, the man sent a photo to the school. The bird was big and healthy, and it was flying. The whole class cheered. Sam kept the photo on his bedroom wall for many years.

=== a2-a-day-without-a-phone | A Day Without My Phone | Technology | A2
Last Sunday, I tried an experiment. I turned off my phone and put it in a drawer for the whole day. I wanted to see what my life was like without it. I was a little nervous, because I use my phone for almost everything.

In the morning, I felt strange. I usually check the news and my messages in bed. Instead, I got up and made breakfast. I read a magazine while I ate. Then I went for a walk in the park. Without my phone, I noticed many things: the birds, the trees, and the people around me.

In the afternoon, I wanted to meet a friend, but I couldn't call her. So I walked to her house and knocked on the door. She was very surprised, but happy. We had coffee and talked for two hours.

In the evening, I turned my phone on again. I had forty messages, but none of them were important. I decided to do this every Sunday.

=== a2-the-wedding | My Cousin's Wedding | Family | A2
Last summer, my cousin Laura got married. The wedding was in a small village in the countryside, where her husband's family lives. More than a hundred and fifty people came, from five different countries.

The ceremony was in an old stone church at two o'clock. Laura wore a long white dress, and she looked beautiful. Her husband, Marco, was so nervous that he forgot some of his words. Everybody laughed, and then he laughed too.

After the ceremony, we went to a big garden for the party. There were long tables under the trees, with flowers and candles. We ate for hours. There was soup, fish, meat, salads, and a huge wedding cake with five layers. Then a band played music, and everybody danced until two in the morning. Even my grandmother danced.

It was a long day, but it was wonderful. I took a lot of photos. My favorite one shows Laura and Marco laughing in the garden.

=== a2-the-gym | Joining a Gym | Health | A2
In January, I decided to get fit. Like many people, I made a New Year's resolution. I joined a gym near my office and paid for a whole year. I was very excited.

For the first two weeks, I went every day. I ran on the machines, lifted weights, and took a yoga class. My whole body hurt, but I felt great. Then work got busy, and I started to miss days. By March, I only went once a week. By May, I didn't go at all.

I felt bad about the money, so in June I tried again. This time, I made a different plan. I found a friend who wanted to go to the gym too. We decided to meet there every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at seven in the morning.

It worked. When you have a partner, it is harder to stay at home. Now, a year later, we still go three times a week. I am stronger, and I have more energy.

=== a2-the-museum-of-science | The Science Museum | Science | A2
Last weekend, I took my two children to the science museum in the city. I wasn't sure they would like it, but it was a great day for all of us.

The museum is very different from a normal museum. You can touch almost everything. In the first room, my son made electricity with a bicycle. My daughter put her hand on a special ball, and her hair stood up. They both laughed a lot.

Upstairs, there was a big room about space. We saw a real rock from the moon and a model of a space station. We sat in a dark room and watched a film about the stars and the planets. My daughter said she wants to be an astronaut.

We stayed for five hours, and we didn't see everything. The children were tired but very happy. In the car, they asked me hundreds of questions. I couldn't answer all of them, so we are going to go back next month.

=== a2-the-cooking-class | The Cooking Class | Hobbies | A2
For my birthday, my husband gave me a cooking class. It was a Thai cooking class at a small restaurant in the city. There were eight students, and our teacher was the chef, a woman from Bangkok called Nok.

First, Nok showed us the ingredients. Some of them were new for me, like lemongrass and fish sauce. She explained how to choose fresh herbs and how to use them. Then we went into the kitchen, and each student had a small cooking station.

We made three dishes: a spicy soup, a green curry, and a noodle dish. Nok walked around and helped everybody. My curry was too spicy at first, so she showed me how to fix it with a little coconut milk. Everything smelled wonderful.

At the end, we sat at a long table and ate our food together. It was delicious. Now I cook Thai food at home every week, and my husband is very happy with his present.

=== a2-an-unusual-pet | An Unusual Pet | Animals | A2
Most people have a cat or a dog. My friend Oliver has a snake. Her name is Bella, and she is about one and a half meters long. She lives in a big glass box in his living room.

When people visit Oliver, they are often scared of Bella. But Oliver says she is very calm and gentle. She is not dangerous, and she doesn't bite. She moves slowly, and she likes to sleep under a warm lamp. She eats only once every week or two.

Oliver says a snake is an easy pet. It doesn't make noise, it doesn't need walks, and it doesn't smell. But it needs the right temperature and a clean home. Oliver checks the temperature in the glass box every day.

The first time I held Bella, I was very nervous. Her skin was dry and smooth, not wet like I expected. Now I am not scared anymore. I still prefer cats, but I understand why Oliver loves his snake.

=== a2-the-village-festival | The Summer Festival | Culture | A2
Every year in August, my grandparents' village has a summer festival. It lasts for three days, and people come from all the villages around. My family goes every year, and I always look forward to it.

During the day, there are games and competitions for children and adults. There is a race in the river, a cake competition, and a big soccer game between two villages. In the main square, people sell local food, like cheese, honey, and fresh bread.

In the evening, there is music and dancing. Long tables stand in the square, and everybody eats dinner together under colored lights. Old people sing traditional songs, and young people dance until late. On the last night, there are fireworks over the lake.

The festival started more than two hundred years ago, to celebrate the end of the harvest. Today, life in the village is very different. But the festival is still the most important event of the year.

=== a2-the-mistake | A Big Mistake | Short Fiction | A2
Peter was the new manager at a small company. On his first Friday, he wanted to send a friendly email to his team. He wrote, "Thank you for a great first week. Let's have lunch together on Monday. I'll pay!"

He also wanted to send a message to his wife about the weekend. He wrote, "I am so tired of this job already. I just want to go home and sleep." Then he pressed send.

Five minutes later, he looked at his screen and went white. He sent the message to his wife to his whole team, and the friendly email to his wife! He didn't know what to do.

On Monday morning, he came to the office very nervous. But when he opened the door, his team was laughing. On his desk, there was a pillow and a cup of coffee with a note: "Sleep well, boss!" Peter laughed too. That lunch was the start of a great team.

=== a2-the-city-at-night | The City at Night | Places | A2
I work at night in a hospital, so I often travel across the city at two or three in the morning. At first, I didn't like it. Now I think the city at night is a special world.

The streets are quiet, and there are only a few cars. The tall buildings are dark, except for a few windows with lights. I sometimes wonder about the people behind those windows. Are they working, reading, or just unable to sleep like me?

At night, I see different people. There are bakers who start work at three o'clock, taxi drivers, and police officers. There are cleaners in the empty offices and young people walking home from parties. There is a small cafe near the station that is open all night. I often stop there for a coffee, and the owner knows my name.

When I finish work, the sky is turning pink, and the city is waking up. Then it is time for me to sleep.

=== a2-my-favorite-teacher | The Best Teacher | School | A2
When I was fourteen, I hated math. I didn't understand it, and my grades were very bad. I thought I was just stupid. Then, in my second year of high school, we got a new math teacher, Mr. Evans.

Mr. Evans was different from the other teachers. He never shouted, and he never made students feel bad. He explained things in many different ways until everybody understood. He used examples from real life, like sports, cooking, and money.

One day, after class, he asked me to stay. I was worried, but he only said, "You are better at math than you think. You just need more confidence." He offered to help me after school on Tuesdays. For the rest of the year, I went to his classroom every week.

At the end of the year, I got the best grade in my class. Today, I am an engineer, and I use math every day. I still think about Mr. Evans and his kind words.
