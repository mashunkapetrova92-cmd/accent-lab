=== a1-moving-day | Moving Day | Home | A1
Today is moving day. My family and I go to a new apartment in another part of the city. There are boxes everywhere. There are boxes in the kitchen, in the hall, and in my room.

A big truck comes at nine o'clock. Two strong men carry our furniture down the stairs. They carry the sofa, the beds, and the big table. My dad helps them, and my mom carries the plants.

The new apartment is bigger than the old one. My new room has a balcony. In the evening, we eat pizza on the floor because the table is still in a box.

=== a1-the-dentist | The Dentist | Health | A1
I go to the dentist two times a year. Today is my appointment. I am a little scared, but my mom comes with me. We sit in the waiting room and read magazines.

The dentist is a young man with a big smile. I sit in a big chair, and I open my mouth. He looks at all my teeth with a small mirror. "Your teeth are very good," he says.

He tells me to brush my teeth two times a day and eat fewer sweets. Then he gives me a new toothbrush. It is green, my favorite color.

=== a1-rainy-weekend-games | Games at Home | Hobbies | A1
It is a rainy weekend, and we can't go outside. My cousins come to our house. There are six children, and we are all bored. Then my aunt has an idea.

She brings old board games from the closet. We play a game with cards and a game with a small house and money. My cousin Tim wins every game, and he is very happy.

Later, we build a big tent in the living room. We use blankets and chairs. We sit inside it with flashlights and tell scary stories. It is the best rainy day.

=== a1-the-kite | The Kite | Short Fiction | A1
Leo has a new kite. It is red and yellow, and it looks like a big bird. It is a windy day, so Leo and his dad go to the top of the hill.

Leo runs, and the kite goes up into the sky. It flies high above the trees. Leo holds the string very tight. Suddenly, the wind is very strong, and the string breaks.

The kite flies away over the hill. Leo is sad. But the next day, a farmer comes to his house with the kite. "Is this your bird?" he asks with a smile.

=== a1-colors-of-the-market | Fruit and Vegetables | Food | A1
I love fruit and vegetables. They have so many colors. Tomatoes and strawberries are red. Bananas and lemons are yellow. Carrots and oranges are orange. Grapes can be green or purple.

Every day, I eat fruit for breakfast. I usually have a banana or an apple. At lunch, I eat a big salad with lettuce, tomatoes, and cucumbers.

My favorite vegetable is the potato. I like potatoes in soup, and I like them hot from the oven. My favorite fruit is the mango. It is sweet and soft.

=== a1-the-taxi-driver | The Taxi Driver | Work | A1
Ahmed is a taxi driver in a big city. He works at night, from seven in the evening to five in the morning. He drives a yellow car, and he knows every street in the city.

Many different people take his taxi. Some go to the airport, and some go home after a party. Some people are quiet, and some talk a lot. Ahmed likes to listen to their stories.

In the morning, Ahmed goes home. His children are going to school, and he is going to bed. He sleeps all day. He doesn't see his family very much, and that is hard.

=== a1-my-cat-luna | My Cat Luna | Animals | A1
My cat is called Luna. She is black with green eyes. She is five years old. Luna is very lazy. She sleeps for about sixteen hours every day.

Luna loves the sun. In the morning, she sleeps by the kitchen window. In the afternoon, she moves to the living room, because the sun is there. She always finds the warm places.

At night, Luna is not lazy. She runs around the house and plays with her toys. Sometimes she jumps on my bed at three o'clock in the morning. I love her, but I want to sleep.

=== a1-the-museum | The Museum | Places | A1
On Saturday, my dad and I go to the city museum. It is a very big building with white walls. Inside, there are old things from many countries and many years.

We see old coins, old clothes, and beautiful paintings. My favorite room has dinosaur bones. There is a very big dinosaur in the middle of the room. It is taller than a house.

We stay in the museum for three hours. Then we eat lunch in the museum cafe. I buy a small dinosaur toy in the shop. I want to come back next month.

=== a1-a-camping-trip | Camping | Travel | A1
This summer, my family goes camping. We drive to a big forest near a lake. We put up our tent between two tall trees. It is green and has space for four people.

In the day, we walk in the forest and swim in the lake. My dad shows me how to make a fire. In the evening, we cook sausages on the fire and eat them with bread.

At night, it is very dark, and I can hear many sounds. I hear owls and the wind in the trees. I am a little scared, but I am warm in my sleeping bag.

=== a1-my-week | My Week | Daily Life | A1
On Mondays and Wednesdays, I go to the gym after work. On Tuesdays, I have a Spanish lesson. On Thursdays, I meet my friends for dinner. I am busy all week.

On Fridays, I am always tired. I go home, cook a simple dinner, and watch a movie. I usually go to bed at ten o'clock.

On Saturdays, I clean my apartment and go shopping. On Sundays, I visit my parents. My mother makes a big lunch, and we talk all afternoon. Then the week starts again.

=== a1-the-lost-key | The Lost Key | Short Fiction | A1
Every day, Mr. Brown puts his key in his coat pocket. But today, when he comes home from work, the key is not there. He looks in all his pockets and in his bag. It is not there.

He sits on the steps in front of his house. It is cold, and it is getting dark. He calls his wife, but she is at work until nine o'clock.

Then his neighbor's little boy comes out. "Mr. Brown, is this your key?" he asks. "It is in the street near the bus stop." Mr. Brown is very happy, and he gives the boy some chocolate.

=== a1-the-hotel | The Hotel | Travel | A1
We are on vacation, and we stay in a hotel by the sea. It is a big white hotel with a swimming pool. Our room is on the fourth floor, and it has a balcony with a view of the sea.

Every morning, we have breakfast in the hotel restaurant. There is fruit, bread, eggs, and pancakes. I eat too much every day.

The people who work in the hotel are very friendly. The man at the front desk helps us with a map of the town. The hotel is perfect, and I don't want to go home.

=== a1-learning-to-swim | Learning to Swim | Hobbies | A1
I am thirty years old, and I can't swim. This year, I want to learn. So every Tuesday evening, I go to a swimming lesson for adults.

There are six people in my class. Our teacher is a young woman called Sara. She is very patient. In the first lesson, we only put our faces in the water. It is not easy for me.

Now it is my eighth lesson. I can swim from one side of the pool to the other. It is only twenty-five meters, but I am very proud. Next summer, I want to swim in the sea.

=== a1-the-tall-tree | The Tall Tree | Nature | A1
In the middle of our village, there is a very tall tree. It is more than two hundred years old. It is taller than all the houses, and its branches are very long.

In summer, the tree gives us shade. Old people sit on the benches under it and talk. Children play around it. Birds make their nests in its branches.

Every winter, the people of the village put lights on the tall tree. In the evening, it is bright and beautiful. Everybody loves the tree. It is the heart of our village.

=== a1-my-classroom | My Classroom | School | A1
My classroom is on the second floor of my school. It is big and bright, with four big windows. There are twenty-four desks and chairs for the students.

At the front of the room, there is a big whiteboard and the teacher's desk. On the walls, there are maps and posters. There are also pictures by the students.

At the back of the classroom, there are shelves with books and games. There is also a small plant on the window. Every week, one student waters it. This week, it is me.

=== a1-the-ice-cream-shop | The Ice Cream Shop | Food | A1
In summer, there is a long line in front of the ice cream shop on our street. The shop has thirty different flavors. There is chocolate, strawberry, lemon, and many more.

My favorite flavor is mint with chocolate chips. My brother always gets vanilla. My mom likes coffee ice cream, and my dad likes everything.

The man in the shop is called Tony. He is from Italy, and he makes all the ice cream himself. He says the secret is fresh milk and lots of love. His ice cream is the best in town.

=== a1-the-sleepy-owl | The Sleepy Owl | Short Fiction | A1
In a tall tree, there lives an owl. In the day, the owl sleeps. In the night, he flies through the forest and looks for food. That is the life of an owl.

But one day, a woodpecker comes to the tree. Knock, knock, knock! The woodpecker is very loud, and the owl can't sleep. "Please stop," says the owl. But the woodpecker doesn't stop.

That night, the owl is very tired. He can't see well, and he can't find food. The next morning, he finds a new tree, far from the woodpecker. Finally, he can sleep.

=== a1-my-neighbor-mrs-white | Mrs. White | People | A1
Mrs. White lives in the apartment under my apartment. She is eighty years old, and she lives alone. Her husband died many years ago, and her children live in other cities.

Mrs. White is very active. She walks in the park every morning and goes to an art class on Thursdays. She has many beautiful plants on her balcony.

On Saturdays, I go shopping for her. I carry her heavy bags up the stairs. Then she makes tea for me, and we talk. She tells me interesting stories about the city long ago.

=== a1-the-car | Our Car | Daily Life | A1
Our car is old and blue. It is fifteen years old, and it is very small. There is space for four people, but only two big bags. My dad loves it.

Every morning, my dad drives the car to work. On the weekend, we go to the lake or to the mountains. The car is slow, and it makes a lot of noise.

Sometimes the car doesn't start, and my dad is angry. My mom wants a new car, but my dad says no. "This car is part of our family," he says.

=== a1-a-snowy-morning | A Snow Day | Weather | A1
This morning, there is a lot of snow. The snow is very deep, and the buses can't go. There is no school today. My sister and I are very happy.

We eat breakfast and put on our warm clothes. We go outside and play in the snow. We make snow angels and throw snowballs at our dad. He throws snowballs at us too.

After two hours, our hands and feet are cold. We go inside, and our mom makes hot chocolate. We sit by the window and watch the snow. It is still falling.
