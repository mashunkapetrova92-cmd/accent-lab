=== a2-the-volunteer | Helping at the Animal Shelter | Animals | A2
Every Saturday morning, I work as a volunteer at an animal shelter near my town. The shelter takes care of dogs and cats that don't have a home. Some of them were lost, and some of them were left by their owners.

When I arrive, I first clean the cages and give the animals fresh water and food. Then I take the dogs for walks, two at a time. Some dogs are very excited and pull hard. Others are scared and walk slowly behind me. I always talk to them quietly.

My favorite dog was an old black dog called Shadow. He was at the shelter for almost a year, because most people want young dogs. Every week, I spent extra time with him. He loved to lie in the sun while I brushed him.

Last month, an older couple came to the shelter. They wanted a calm dog, and they chose Shadow. I was sad to say goodbye, but very happy for him. Now he has a family.

=== a2-the-first-flight | My First Flight | Travel | A2
I didn't fly on a plane until I was thirty. I was always afraid of flying. When my company sent me to a meeting in another country, I didn't have a choice. I had to go.

The night before the flight, I couldn't sleep. At the airport, my hands were wet, and my heart was beating fast. On the plane, I sat next to a young woman with a baby. She saw I was nervous and started talking to me. She told me she flew every month for her job.

When the plane took off, I closed my eyes and held the seat very tight. The noise was very loud, and my stomach felt strange. But after a few minutes, everything was calm. I opened my eyes and looked out of the window. We were above the clouds, and the sun was shining. It was beautiful.

The flight took three hours. When we landed, I felt very proud. I still don't love flying, but now I am not afraid.

=== a2-the-secret-recipe | The Secret Recipe | Short Fiction | A2
In a small town, there was a restaurant famous for its tomato soup. People came from other cities just to try it. The owner, an old woman called Rosa, never told anybody the secret recipe.

When Rosa got older, she wanted to retire. Her grandson, Luca, wanted to take over the restaurant. "Please, Grandma," he said, "tell me the secret of your soup." Rosa smiled. "I will show you," she said. "Come to the kitchen tomorrow at six in the morning."

The next morning, Luca watched carefully. Rosa used fresh tomatoes, onions, garlic, olive oil, and a little salt. Nothing special. She cooked the soup very slowly for three hours and tasted it many times. "But what is the secret ingredient?" asked Luca.

Rosa laughed. "Time," she said. "And I make it for people I love. Everybody who comes here is family." Luca understood. Today, he runs the restaurant, and the soup is still famous.

=== a2-living-without-a-car | Living Without a Car | Environment | A2
Three years ago, my car broke down, and the repair was very expensive. I decided not to buy a new car. My friends said I was crazy, but it was one of the best decisions of my life.

At first, it was difficult. I had to plan my trips carefully. I learned the bus times, and I bought a good bicycle. For long trips, I take the train. When I really need a car, for example to move furniture, I rent one for a day.

Now I save a lot of money every month. I don't pay for gas, parking, repairs, or insurance. I also get more exercise, because I walk and ride my bike every day. I feel healthier, and I lost five kilos in the first year.

There are some disadvantages, of course. In winter, it is not fun to wait for a bus in the rain. And it is harder to visit my parents in the countryside. But for me, life without a car is simpler and calmer.

=== a2-the-hiking-trip | Lost in the Forest | Nature | A2
Last autumn, my friend Kate and I went hiking in a big national park. We planned a walk of fifteen kilometers. We had a map, water, sandwiches, and warm jackets. The weather was sunny and cool.

For the first three hours, everything was perfect. We walked through forests of tall trees and saw deer and a big eagle. But in the afternoon, we took a wrong path. After an hour, we didn't recognize anything, and our phones didn't have a signal. It was starting to get dark.

We were scared, but we tried to stay calm. Kate remembered that rivers usually go down to villages. We found a small river and walked next to it. It was cold, and we were very tired and hungry.

After two more hours, we saw lights. It was a small farmhouse. A friendly farmer and his wife gave us hot tea and drove us back to our car. Now I always tell somebody my plans before I go hiking.

=== a2-the-singing-teacher | Singing Lessons | Hobbies | A2
I always loved singing, but I only sang in the shower or in the car. I thought my voice was terrible. Then, on my fortieth birthday, my husband gave me five singing lessons as a present.

My teacher was a woman called Helen. She was about sixty, and she sang in an opera choir for many years. In the first lesson, I was so nervous that my voice was very quiet. Helen laughed kindly. "Everybody can sing," she said. "You just need to breathe."

She taught me how to stand, how to breathe from my stomach, and how to open my mouth. We practiced simple songs and exercises. Every week, my voice got a little stronger. After the five lessons, I bought ten more.

Now I sing in a choir with thirty other people. We practice every Thursday evening and give concerts at Christmas and in the summer. Singing makes me happy, and I am not afraid of my voice anymore.

=== a2-the-old-castle | The Old Castle | History | A2
On a hill above my town, there is an old stone castle. It is more than eight hundred years old. Today, it is a museum, and many tourists visit it every summer.

The castle was built by a rich family to protect the town from enemies. It has thick walls, four tall towers, and a deep ditch around it. In the past, the only way in was across a wooden bridge. When enemies came, the soldiers pulled the bridge up.

Inside the castle, you can see the great hall, where the family ate big dinners, and the small rooms where they slept. In winter, it was very cold, even with big fires. There is also a dark room under the ground. It was a prison, and it is a little scary.

My favorite part is the top of the highest tower. From there, you can see the whole town, the river, and the mountains. On a clear day, you can see for more than fifty kilometers.

=== a2-a-difficult-customer | A Difficult Customer | Work | A2
Last year, I worked in a clothes shop in a shopping center. Most of the customers were nice, but one day, a woman came in who was very angry. She had a shopping bag with a dress in it.

She shouted, "This dress is terrible! I wore it once, and now there is a hole in it. I want my money back!" Everybody in the shop looked at us. I felt my face go red. I wanted to shout back, but I remembered my manager's advice: always stay calm and listen.

So I said, "I am very sorry. Can I see the dress, please?" I looked at it carefully. There was a small hole in the side. I told her I could give her a new dress or her money. Slowly, she became calmer. She chose a new dress and even said thank you.

A week later, she came back and bought three more things. She was smiling. I learned that angry people often just want somebody to listen.

=== a2-the-photo-competition | The Photo Competition | Hobbies | A2
Last year, my town had a photo competition. The theme was "Our Town in Winter." The prize was a new camera. I am not a professional photographer, but I love taking photos, so I decided to try.

Every weekend in December and January, I walked around the town with my old camera. I took photos of the snowy park, the frozen river, and the market with its colorful lights. I took more than five hundred photos, but most of them were not very good.

One cold morning, I saw an old man feeding birds in the park. The snow was falling, and the light was soft and golden. I took the photo quickly. When I looked at it at home, I knew it was special.

I sent that photo to the competition. A month later, I got a phone call. I won second prize! My photo was in the town newspaper and in an exhibition at the library. I didn't win the camera, but I was very happy.

=== a2-the-lighthouse | The Lighthouse | Places | A2
On a small rock in the sea, near the coast where I grew up, there is an old white lighthouse. It is about thirty meters tall, and it was built more than a hundred and fifty years ago.

For many years, a lighthouse keeper lived there with his family. His job was to keep the light on every night, so that ships didn't hit the rocks in the dark. It was a lonely life. In bad weather, the family couldn't leave the lighthouse for weeks. The children went to school on the mainland by boat.

Today, the light is automatic, and nobody lives there. A computer turns it on and off. But in summer, small boats take tourists to visit it. You can climb the two hundred steps to the top and look at the sea.

When I was a child, I could see the light from my bedroom window. Every night, it flashed every ten seconds. It always made me feel safe.

=== a2-the-best-friend | Friends Forever | People | A2
I met my best friend, Lisa, on our first day of school. We were six years old. I was crying because I missed my mother, and Lisa gave me a piece of her chocolate. From that day, we were always together.

We went to the same school for twelve years. We did our homework together, we laughed at the same jokes, and we told each other all our secrets. Sometimes we argued, but never for long. After school, Lisa went to a university in another city, and I started working. We were sad, but we called each other every week.

Now we are both thirty-five. Lisa lives in another country with her husband and her two children. We don't see each other often, maybe once a year. But when we meet, it feels like we were never apart. We talk for hours, just like when we were children.

I have many friends, but Lisa is special. She knows me better than anybody.

=== a2-the-flood | The Flood | Weather | A2
Two years ago, it rained in our town for six days without stopping. The river next to the town got higher and higher. On the sixth night, the water came over the river banks and into the streets.

My family lives near the river. At three o'clock in the morning, my father woke us up. "Get up! The water is coming!" he shouted. We quickly carried important things upstairs: photos, papers, and the computer. The water came into our house and covered the whole ground floor. It was almost one meter deep.

In the morning, a boat came to take us to a school on the hill. There were many families there. People brought blankets, food, and dry clothes. Nobody was hurt, but everybody was very worried about their homes.

After a week, the water went down. Our house was dirty and wet, and we lost a lot of furniture. It took months to repair everything. But our neighbors helped us, and we helped them.

=== a2-the-language-of-bees | How Bees Talk | Science | A2
Bees are very small, but they are amazing animals. They live together in big groups called colonies. One colony can have more than fifty thousand bees. Each bee has a job, and they all work together.

Scientists discovered something surprising about bees. They can "talk" to each other with a special dance. When a bee finds a lot of flowers, it flies back to the hive. Then it dances in a special way. The dance tells the other bees where the flowers are and how far away they are.

If the flowers are close, the bee dances in a small circle. If they are far away, it dances in a line and moves its body from side to side. The direction of the dance shows the direction of the flowers. The other bees watch, and then they fly straight to the food.

Bees are very important for people too. They help many plants grow fruit and vegetables. Without bees, we would have much less food.

=== a2-the-new-apartment | Our New Apartment | Home | A2
Last month, my boyfriend and I moved into our first apartment together. It is on the third floor of an old building in the city center. It has two bedrooms, a small kitchen, and a living room with a big window.

The apartment was not in good condition when we got it. The walls were dark and dirty, and the kitchen was very old. So we spent three weekends painting and cleaning. We painted the living room light blue and the bedroom white. My father helped us fix the kitchen door.

We didn't have much money for furniture, so we bought some things secondhand. We found a beautiful old table at a market and a comfortable sofa online. My grandmother gave us some plates and glasses. Our friends gave us plants.

Now the apartment feels like home. It is not perfect. The heating is noisy, and the shower is very small. But we love it. On Sunday mornings, we sit by the window, drink coffee, and watch the street.

=== a2-the-runaway-balloon | The Red Balloon | Short Fiction | A2
At the town fair, a little girl called Amy got a big red balloon. She was very happy, and she held the string very tight. But when she was eating her ice cream, she forgot, and the balloon flew away.

Amy started to cry. The balloon went up over the trees and the houses, higher and higher, until it was only a small red dot in the sky. Her father bought her a new balloon, but she didn't want it. She wanted her red balloon.

Many kilometers away, an old farmer was working in his field. He looked up and saw something red in a tree. It was a balloon, and there was a small card on the string. On the card, it said, "My name is Amy. Please write to me." Her mother wrote the address there.

A week later, Amy got a letter with a photo of the farmer and his cows. They became pen pals, and every summer, Amy visited the farm.

=== a2-a-day-at-the-office | Working from Home | Work | A2
Since last year, I work from home three days a week. On the other two days, I go to the office. At first, I loved working from home. I didn't have to take the crowded train, and I could work in comfortable clothes.

But after a few months, I noticed some problems. I started work earlier and finished later, because my office was always there, in my living room. I didn't take real breaks for lunch. And I felt lonely, because I didn't talk to my colleagues very much.

So I made some rules. I start work at nine o'clock and finish at five. I have a real lunch break, and I go for a short walk outside. When I finish work, I close my computer and put it in a drawer. I also call a colleague every day, just to chat.

Now I enjoy both. At home, I can concentrate better. At the office, I can see my friends and share ideas.

=== a2-the-music-festival | The Music Festival | Culture | A2
Last summer, I went to a big music festival with three friends. It lasted for four days, and more than fifty thousand people came. There were five stages and more than a hundred bands.

We camped in a big field with thousands of other people. Our tent was small, and the ground was hard. On the second night, it rained a lot, and everything got wet and muddy. My new white shoes were brown at the end of the festival.

But the music was fantastic. We saw our favorite band on the main stage on Saturday night. They played for two hours, and everybody sang along. We also discovered some new bands that we didn't know before. Between concerts, we ate food from different countries and danced.

When I got home, I was very tired and very dirty. I slept for fourteen hours. But it was one of the best weeks of my life. Next year, I am definitely going again.

=== a2-the-tooth-fairy | Traditions for Lost Teeth | Culture | A2
When children lose their first teeth, many families have special traditions. These traditions are different in different countries, and some of them are quite strange.

In the United States, children put their tooth under their pillow at night. They believe that the tooth fairy comes while they are asleep. She takes the tooth and leaves some money. In many Spanish-speaking countries, it isn't a fairy, but a little mouse who takes the tooth.

In some countries in Asia, children throw their teeth. If it is a tooth from the top of the mouth, they throw it down to the ground. If it is from the bottom, they throw it up onto the roof. People believe this helps the new tooth grow straight and strong.

In other places, children give their teeth to an animal, or their parents keep them in a small box. All these traditions help children feel brave and happy about something that can be a little scary.

=== a2-the-chef | My Life as a Chef | Work | A2
I have worked as a chef for fifteen years. Now I have my own small restaurant, but I started in a big hotel kitchen when I was eighteen. My first job was washing vegetables and cleaning the floors.

Life in a kitchen is hard. You work long hours, often twelve hours a day. You work in the evenings, on the weekends, and on holidays, when other people are relaxing. The kitchen is hot and noisy, and everybody is in a hurry. Sometimes the head chef shouted at me, and I wanted to quit.

But I loved cooking, so I stayed. I watched the other chefs and learned from them. After a few years, I became a chef myself. Then I worked in restaurants in three different countries. I learned a lot about different kinds of food.

Today, my restaurant is small, with only twelve tables. I cook food from my grandmother's village. When people enjoy my food, I feel that all the hard work was worth it.

=== a2-the-sleepy-town | The Town That Sleeps | Places | A2
In some countries in southern Europe, many small towns have a tradition called the siesta. In the afternoon, from about two o'clock to five o'clock, shops close and the streets are empty. People go home to eat lunch and rest.

The siesta started a long time ago, because the afternoons in summer are very hot. It was too hot to work outside in the fields. So people worked early in the morning and again in the evening, when it was cooler.

For tourists, the siesta can be a surprise. Last summer, I visited a small town in Spain. At three o'clock, I wanted to buy some water, but every shop was closed. The town was so quiet that I could hear the birds. In the evening, everything opened again, and the streets were full of families until midnight.

Today, many people in big cities don't take a siesta anymore. But in small towns, the tradition is still alive.
