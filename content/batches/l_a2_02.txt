=== a2-the-night-train | The Night Train | Travel | A2
Last year, I traveled from one side of the country to the other by night train. The trip took fourteen hours. Many people asked me, "Why don't you fly? It's much faster." But I wanted an adventure.

The train left at seven in the evening. I had a small cabin with a bed, a tiny sink, and a window. I shared it with an old woman called Edith. She was traveling to see her new grandson. She had a big bag of food, and she shared her sandwiches and cookies with me.

In the evening, we sat in the restaurant car and watched the sun go down over the fields. Then I went to bed. The train moved slowly from side to side, and the sound of the wheels was relaxing. I slept better than I do at home.

When I woke up, we were in the mountains. There was snow on the trees, and the sky was bright blue. It was the most beautiful morning I can remember.

=== a2-the-recycling-project | Our Recycling Project | Environment | A2
Last year, our class started a recycling project at school. Our teacher showed us a photo of our school's trash. Every week, we threw away more than two hundred plastic bottles and a lot of paper. Most of it went into the normal trash.

We decided to change this. First, we made colorful boxes for plastic, paper, and cans, and we put them in every classroom. Then we made posters to explain the project. Every Friday, two students checked the boxes and took them to the big recycling bins behind the school.

At first, many students forgot, and they still put everything in the normal trash. So we visited every class and talked about the project. We showed them what happens to plastic in the sea. After a month, things got much better.

At the end of the year, our school recycled four times more than before. The town newspaper wrote a story about us. We were very proud.

=== a2-the-broken-leg | The Broken Leg | Health | A2
Last winter, I went skiing with my friends for the first time. On the first day, we took a lesson, and I learned the basics. On the second day, I felt confident, so I went down a more difficult slope. That was a big mistake.

I was going too fast, and I couldn't stop. I hit a small hill and fell. I heard a terrible sound, and then I felt a lot of pain in my leg. I couldn't stand up. My friends called for help, and two men came with a special sled and took me down the mountain.

At the hospital, the doctor said my leg was broken. I had a big white cast for six weeks. I couldn't drive, I couldn't go up the stairs easily, and I couldn't take a normal shower. Everything was difficult.

But my friends were wonderful. They brought me food and played games with me. Now my leg is fine, and next year I am going to try skiing again, but more slowly.

=== a2-the-mystery-letter | The Mystery Letter | Short Fiction | A2
One morning, Clara found a strange letter in her mailbox. There was no stamp and no name on the envelope. Inside, there was a small piece of paper with only one sentence: "Go to the old oak tree in the park at four o'clock."

Clara was curious, but also a little worried. Who wrote it? And why? She showed it to her brother, Max. "It's probably a joke," he said. But at half past three, they both walked to the park.

The old oak tree was in the middle of the park, next to the lake. There was nobody there. Then Clara saw something in a hole in the tree. It was a small box with a red ribbon. Inside, there was a key and another note: "Happy birthday, Clara. Look in the garage."

Clara ran home and opened the garage. There was a new blue bike with a big bow on it. Her parents were standing next to it, smiling. She forgot it was her birthday!

=== a2-the-market-in-morocco | A Market in Morocco | Travel | A2
Two years ago, I visited Marrakech, a city in Morocco. The most exciting place in the city was the big market in the old town. It is a maze of narrow streets with hundreds of small shops.

When I walked in, I didn't know where to look. There were colorful carpets, leather bags, silver lamps, and piles of spices in every color. Men were cooking meat on small grills, and the air smelled of cinnamon and smoke. People were shouting, music was playing, and motorbikes were driving through the crowds.

In the market, you don't pay the first price. You have to discuss the price with the seller. At first, I was embarrassed, but then I started to enjoy it. I bought a beautiful lamp for half the first price. The seller and I drank mint tea together and laughed.

I got lost three times, but every time, somebody helped me find my way. I will never forget that market.

=== a2-a-letter-from-grandma | A Letter from Grandma | Family | A2
My grandmother doesn't use email or a phone. She still writes letters by hand. Every month, I receive a long letter from her in her small, neat handwriting.

She writes about her garden, her neighbors, and her cat. She tells me about the books she is reading and the recipes she is trying. Sometimes she puts a dried flower or an old photo in the envelope. Her letters always smell a little of lavender.

At first, I answered her letters with a quick phone call. But last year, I started to write letters back. It was strange at first. I haven't written by hand for years, and my writing was messy. But I enjoy it now. When I write a letter, I think more carefully about my words.

I keep all my grandmother's letters in a wooden box. There are more than fifty now. I know that one day they will be very precious to me.

=== a2-the-quiet-neighbor | The Quiet Neighbor | People | A2
For three years, I lived next to a man called Mr. Novak. He was very quiet. He said "good morning" when we met on the stairs, but nothing more. I didn't know anything about him.

Then, one evening, the electricity went off in our whole building. It was very dark, and I didn't have any candles. I knocked on Mr. Novak's door to ask for help. He opened it with a candle in his hand and invited me in.

His apartment was full of musical instruments: violins, guitars, and an old piano. He told me he was a music teacher for forty years. While we waited for the electricity, he played the piano for me by candlelight. It was beautiful.

After that night, we became friends. Every Sunday, I visited him for tea, and he told me about his life and his travels. He moved away last year to live with his daughter, but we still write to each other. I learned that quiet people often have the best stories.

=== a2-the-football-match | The Final Game | Hobbies | A2
Last season, my daughter's soccer team got to the final of the city competition. The girls were only eleven years old, but they played very well all year. Nobody expected them to get so far.

The final was on a cold Saturday morning in March. The other team was bigger and stronger, and they won the competition the year before. At half time, our team was losing two goals to zero. The girls were sad and tired, and some of them were crying.

Their coach talked to them quietly. She didn't shout. She told them she was proud of them, and that they had nothing to lose. In the second half, the girls played like a different team. They scored one goal, and then another. Two minutes before the end, my daughter scored the third goal.

The parents went crazy. We jumped, shouted, and hugged each other. The girls won three goals to two, and they lifted the cup together. I will never forget their faces.

=== a2-the-weekend-job | Working at the Zoo | Work | A2
For two summers, when I was a university student, I worked at the city zoo. My job was to help the animal keepers. I wanted to be a vet, so it was perfect for me.

The work started at seven o'clock in the morning. First, we cleaned the animals' homes. It was dirty and smelly work, and I was always wet. Then we prepared the food. Every animal had a special diet. The monkeys ate fruit and vegetables, and the lions ate meat. The penguins ate fish, of course.

My favorite animals were the elephants. There were three of them, and each one had a different personality. The oldest one, Rosie, was very calm. She knew all the keepers, and she always touched my arm with her trunk when I said hello.

The job wasn't easy, and the money wasn't great. But I learned so much about animals. Today I am a vet, and I still visit the zoo sometimes.

=== a2-the-best-day-of-the-year | New Year's Eve | Culture | A2
In my family, New Year's Eve is the most important party of the year. Every year on December thirty-first, my whole family meets at my parents' house. There are usually about twenty people, from babies to great-grandparents.

We start cooking in the afternoon. My mother makes her famous salad, my aunt brings a big roast chicken, and my grandmother bakes three different cakes. The children decorate the house with balloons and lights. In the evening, we sit at a long table and eat for hours.

At about half past eleven, we write our wishes for the new year on small pieces of paper. We don't show them to anybody. Just before midnight, we turn on the television and count down the last ten seconds together. Then everybody hugs and kisses, and we go outside to watch the fireworks.

We usually go to bed at three or four in the morning. The next day is always very quiet.

=== a2-the-garden-on-the-roof | A Garden on the Roof | Environment | A2
I live in an apartment in the city center, and I don't have a garden. For years, I wanted to grow my own vegetables. Then, two years ago, my neighbors and I had an idea. Why not use the flat roof of our building?

We asked the building owner, and he said yes. We bought big wooden boxes and filled them with soil. In spring, we planted tomatoes, lettuce, beans, and herbs. Everybody in the building helped. Some people watered the plants, and some people pulled out the weeds.

That first summer, we had more vegetables than we could eat. We shared them with everybody in the building. We also put a table and some chairs on the roof. In the evenings, we often eat dinner there together and watch the sun go down over the city.

Before the garden, I didn't know most of my neighbors. Now they are my friends. The garden gave us more than vegetables.

=== a2-the-old-photograph | A Photo from the Past | History | A2
In my parents' living room, there is a black and white photo of a busy street. It shows our town about a hundred years ago. I often look at it and try to imagine life at that time.

In the photo, there are no cars. People are walking in the middle of the street, and there is a horse and cart outside a shop. The women are wearing long dresses and big hats, and the men are wearing dark suits. A group of children is playing with a ball near the church.

The church is still there today, and so are some of the buildings. But most things are very different now. The street is full of traffic, and there are big supermarkets and banks instead of small shops.

My great-grandfather is in the photo. He is the young man standing in front of the bakery. He owned that bakery for thirty years. Today, it is a phone shop.

=== a2-the-runner | My First Race | Health | A2
Two years ago, I couldn't run for more than five minutes. I was always tired, and I didn't do any exercise. Then my best friend asked me to run a ten kilometer race with her. I laughed. "Me? I can't run," I said. But she didn't give up.

We started training together in January. On the first day, we ran for one minute and walked for two minutes, ten times. It was very hard. Every week, we ran a little more and walked a little less. After two months, I could run for thirty minutes without stopping.

The race was in June. On the day, I was very nervous. There were thousands of people, music, and crowds. The first five kilometers were OK, but then it got very hot, and my legs hurt.

My friend stayed with me the whole time. When we saw the finish line, we held hands and ran together. It took us one hour and five minutes. I cried at the end. I was so proud.

=== a2-the-strange-noise | The Strange Noise | Short Fiction | A2
For a week, Tom and his wife heard a strange noise in their house every night. It started at about midnight. It was a quiet scratching sound, and it came from the wall in the kitchen.

"It's a mouse," said Tom. They put food out, but nothing happened. The next night, the noise was louder. Tom's wife couldn't sleep. "Maybe it's a ghost," she said, only half joking. The house was very old, and some neighbors told strange stories about it.

On Saturday, Tom decided to find out. He moved the big kitchen cupboard away from the wall. Behind it, there was a small hole. He put a flashlight in the hole and looked inside. Two shining eyes looked back at him.

It wasn't a mouse or a ghost. It was a mother cat with four tiny kittens. She probably came in through a broken window in the basement. Tom and his wife kept one of the kittens. They called him Ghost.

=== a2-visiting-new-york | A Week in New York | Travel | A2
Last spring, I spent a week in New York with my best friend. It was our first time in the United States. We saved money for a long time, and we were very excited.

The city was bigger and busier than I imagined. The buildings were so tall that my neck hurt from looking up. There were people everywhere, speaking many different languages. On the first day, we walked for eight hours and saw only a small part of the city.

We did many famous things. We visited the Statue of Liberty and took the boat across the harbor. We walked across the Brooklyn Bridge at sunset. We spent a whole day in Central Park and ate hot dogs from a street cart. In the evening, we went to the top of a tall building and looked at the lights of the city.

New York was expensive, and it was very noisy. But it was exciting, and I loved it. I want to go back one day.

=== a2-the-repair-cafe | The Repair Cafe | Society | A2
Once a month, there is a repair cafe in the community center near my home. People bring broken things, and volunteers help them repair them for free. You can drink coffee and eat cake while you wait.

Last month, I took my old lamp and a jacket with a broken zipper. There were about thirty people there. A retired engineer looked at my lamp. He showed me the problem and helped me change a small part. In ten minutes, it worked again. Then a woman with a sewing machine fixed my zipper.

Many people throw things away when they break. It is often cheaper to buy something new. But this creates a lot of waste, and it is bad for the environment. At the repair cafe, people learn how to repair their things themselves.

I enjoyed the afternoon very much. I learned something new, and I met interesting people. Next month, I am going to go again. Maybe I will be a volunteer too.

=== a2-the-birthday-cake | The Birthday Cake | Food | A2
When my son was five, he asked for a special birthday cake. He wanted a cake that looked like a dinosaur. A cake like that costs a lot of money in a shop, so I decided to make it myself.

I found a recipe on the internet and bought everything I needed. On the night before the party, I started baking. I made two big chocolate cakes and let them cool. Then I tried to cut them into the shape of a dinosaur. It was much harder than it looked in the video.

My first dinosaur looked like a potato. My second dinosaur looked like a potato with legs. At two o'clock in the morning, I was tired and angry. I covered the whole cake with green icing and put some candy on it for eyes and teeth.

The next day, I was worried. But when my son saw the cake, he shouted, "A dinosaur!" and hugged me. All the children loved it. It was not perfect, but it was made with love.

=== a2-the-tree-house | The Tree House | Family | A2
When I was nine years old, my father built a tree house for me and my brother. It was in an old apple tree at the bottom of our garden. It took him four weekends, and we helped him carry the wood.

The tree house was small, with one window and a door. To get in, you had to climb a rope ladder. Inside, there was space for two children and a small table. We put old cushions on the floor and pictures on the walls.

That tree house became our secret place. We read comics there, ate snacks, and made plans for adventures. In summer, we sometimes slept there with our sleeping bags. We could hear the owls at night, and it was a little scary, but exciting.

Many years later, my parents sold the house. The new family has two small children. Last summer, I drove past, and I saw two little faces in the window of the tree house. It made me very happy.

=== a2-the-chess-club | The Chess Club | Hobbies | A2
At my school, there is a chess club every Wednesday after lessons. I joined it last year because my friend Leo was a member. I didn't really know how to play. I only knew how the pieces moved.

The club teacher, Mrs. Patel, is a very good player. She taught us the basic rules and some simple strategies. She told us to always think before we move and to look at the whole board. In my first games, I lost very quickly, sometimes in only ten moves.

But I didn't give up. I played chess on my computer at home and read a book about famous games. Slowly, I started to win some games. Chess taught me to be patient and to think about the future, not only about the next move.

In March, our club played in a competition against other schools. I won four games and lost two. Our team came second. Now chess is my favorite hobby.

=== a2-the-small-island | Life on a Small Island | Places | A2
My friend Sara lives on a very small island. Only about three hundred people live there. There are no cars on the island, only bicycles and a few small tractors. To get to the mainland, you have to take a boat. It takes forty minutes.

Life on the island is very simple. There is one shop, one school, one doctor, and one cafe. Everybody knows everybody. When somebody is sick or needs help, the neighbors are always there. Children play outside all day, because it is very safe.

But life there is not always easy. In winter, there are big storms, and sometimes the boat can't go for days. Then there is no fresh food in the shop. Many young people leave the island when they finish school, because there are not many jobs.

Sara says she would never live anywhere else. She loves the quiet, the sea, and the people. When I visit her, I understand why.
